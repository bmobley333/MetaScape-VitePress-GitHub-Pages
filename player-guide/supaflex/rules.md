# 🌌 SupaFlex Rules

**Last updated:** 2026-10-13 (Player-First Rules Edition)

## Welcome to SupaFlex

### How to Use This Book

* **New players:** read Parts 1–4 in order, then build a hero with §2.5.
* Section numbers (e.g. §4.5) are used for cross-references, so each rule lives in one place.
* Look up any term or symbol in Appendix C, and keep Appendix D (Quick Reference Sheet) at the table.

### What Makes SupaFlex Different

* **Cinematic —** Big swing dice (d20 + d(Atr)) where d(Atr) explode, creates dramatic highs and lows that feel like movie moments. Every roll should carry weight, producing stories worth retelling.
* **Fast —** Rules are built for speed: creation, advancement, and play should never bog down. When in doubt, simplify to keep the momentum alive.
* **Open —** Any concept works with GM approval; SupaFlex thrives on unusual ideas, and the rules bend to fit imagination, not restrict it.
* **Inclusive —** Anyone can attempt weapons, armor, shields, or skills. Unskilled = 1d20, Skilled = 2H20 (2d20 keep the highest); but Powers 🔥 must be learned. This ensures new players can always contribute and veterans can experiment.
* **Tactical —** Defense split (Block, Dodge, or Resist) forces meaningful, moment-to-moment choices. Every defense is a gamble that shapes combat’s flow.
* **GM-Light —** GM sets difficulties and advantage/disadvantage; no dice rolling other than the GM Intensity die. This frees the GM to run the story and spotlight creativity instead of crunching math.
* **Unified —** Attributes are derived into and govern ALL skills, damage, armor, etc.; one backbone supports all mechanics. Everything connects cleanly, making the system easy to teach and expand.
* **Clean —** Clear action types (AM, A, M, P, F) and Atr tags keep the table legible. Players should always know what they can do at a glance.
* **Minimal Tracking —** Players should track only Vit and Luck whenever possible. Low overhead means more energy spent on roleplay, tactics, and fun.
* **Standardized Usage Options —** Every ability uses exactly this 6-option list and order: 1-⚡ (1 Bolt), 1-🍀 (1 Luck), 1-Enc, 2-Enc, 3-Enc, 1-Rnd. Finite numerical charges (3, 2, 1) are strictly reserved for physical Chaos Gauntlet Gems (which crack and auto-shatter upon using the 3rd charge).

## Part 1 · Core Rules: The One Roll

Everything in SupaFlex runs on one roll. Learn it here; the rest of the book builds on it.

### 1.1 Dice You Need

* Dice notation follows standard TTRPG shorthand. For example, 2d8+12 means roll two eight-sided dice, add the results together, then add +12.
* SupaFlex uses the classic RPG dice: d4, d6, d8, d10, d12, and d20 (three d20 are useful for Skilled rolls with Adv).
* SupaFlex also uses the d100, rolled with two d10 (one for tens, one for ones).
* **Example:** a roll of 8 and 2 = 82; a roll of 0 and 8 = 8; a roll of 0 and 0 = 100.
* Some d10 use 0–9, others 1–10. In either case, treat 10 as 0.
* **3H20 →** Roll three d20, keep the highest.
* **2L20 →** Roll two d20, keep the lowest.
* +#d / –#d → Increase or decrease the die type by # steps, within d4, d6, d8, d10, d12.
* **Example:** d6 +1d → d8; d10 –2d → d6.
* d4 is always the minimum; d12 is always the maximum.
* **Odd Dice (d7, d32, etc.) →** Roll the next higher die type, reroll results above the desired range.
* **Examples:** d2 → use d4 (reroll 3–4), d7 → use d8 (reroll 8), d24 or d32 → use d100. For d14: roll a d20 until you get 1–14, reroll 15+.
* If any die is cocked, rolls off the table, is the wrong type, or you forgot a die: reroll the ENTIRE set.

### 1.2 The Ability Roll

All Ability checks compare your roll total against a target **Difficulty (Dif)** such as enemy **Defense / Attack**:

| Roll Type | Dice Formula | Typical Usage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Unskilled Check** | 1d20 + d(Atr) | Untrained skills, weapons, armor, or shields. |
| **Skilled Check** | 2H20 + d(Atr) (roll 2d20, keep highest) | Trained skills, weapon, armor, shields, Powers 🔥, and Exotics 🧿. |
| **Skilled with Advantage** | 3H20 + d(Atr) (roll 3d20, keep highest) | Favorable circumstances, tactical positioning, or ally assists. |
| **Skilled with Disadvantage** | 1d20 + d(Atr) | Penalized conditions, poor visibility. |
| **Unskilled with Disadvantage** | 2L20 + d(Atr) (roll 2d20, keep lowest) | Untrained check under adverse tactical conditions. |

**An Ability Roll (also called Ability Check) is:**

* #d20 + d(Atr) + Bonus vs. Dif
* **#d20:** 2H20 if Skilled, 1d20 if Unskilled. Other options exist for Adv/Dis (see below). Note: ALL ability roll’s d(Atr) dice are exploding. But the d20s NEVER explode.
* **d(Atr) or d💪, d🏃, d👁️, d✨, or d🫀:** Roll the base d(Atr) of the relevant Attribute — (e.g. a simple d4, d6, d8, d10, or d12). Note: these explode.
* **Bonus & Penalty (±10 Modifier Cap):** Flat situational, gear ⚙️, trait 🧬, power 🔥, or condition modifiers combine up to a hard cap of +10 and -10. A penalty to a d(Atr) or check may not reduce the roll below 0; e.g. a d✨–2 where d✨ rolls 1 (math of -1) results in 0.
* Focus Die are die additions, and do not touch the ±10 modifier cap and explode.
* **Example:** A Skilled dagger Atk with 💪 of d8 and +1 bonus:
  * **2H20 (rolled 3, 17) →** keep 17; d8 (rolled 5); +1 Bonus.
  * Result = 17 + 5 + 1 = 23.

#### Difficulty Ladder

* Easy = 10
* Medium = 15
* Hard = 20
* Very Hard = 25
* **Extreme = 28+ Note:** Ties always go to PCs.

#### 1.2.1 You Roll, the GM Doesn't (Caret ^ Notation)

In SupaFlex, **ONLY PLAYERS ROLL DICE**. Monsters and non-player targets never roll saving throws, defense checks, or any other dice. Hostile offensive maneuvers, status conditions, and afflictions are framed strictly from the player's perspective, testing the player's active roll against the target's static attribute rating using Caret (^) notation:

[PC Roll]^[Target Static Atr] or [Affliction/Condition]

* **Wrestling / Restraint Contest:** 💪^💪 or target Restrained
* **Tripping / Knockdown:** 💪^🏃 or target Prone
* **Mental Disorientation:** 👁️^👁️ or target Stunned
* **Stamina / Toxin Onset:** 🫀^🫀 or target Incapacitated

#### 1.2.2 Bonuses Don't Stack

**Master Stacking Rule —** There is a +1 bonus from holding a full Bolt ⚡ that stacks with nearly all other rolls (subject to GM discretion). ALL other bonuses, buffs, power amplifiers, and numerical modifiers DO NOT STACK unless an ability explicitly states "stacks with..." or with explicit GM approval. When multiple passive buffs or powers offer competing bonuses to the same roll or trait, the player may pick only one of them (obviously this will typically be the single highest bonus).

### 1.3 Advantage & Disadvantage

**Examples below use:** Sword Atk ⚔️, Might 💪 d8, +1 bonus.

* **Skilled/Unskilled:**
  * **Unskilled →** 1d20 + d8 + 1.
  * **Skilled →** 2H20 + d8 + 1.
* **Advantage →** Add one extra d20, keep highest.
  * **If Unskilled →** 2H20 + d8 + 1.
  * **If Skilled →** 3H20 + d8 + 1.
* **Disadvantage →** Roll one fewer d20.
  * **If Unskilled →** 2L20 + d8 + 1.
  * **If Skilled →** 1d20 + d8 + 1.

**Note:** There is no such thing as double or triple advantage or disadvantage. Once you have advantage additional indications of advantage do not stack - they simply confirm the ONE advantage. Same for disadvantage. If a character has both then they cancel in equal numbers. So in a super rare 2 advantages and one disadvantage scenario there would be ONE advantage.

#### 🎲 How Monster Advantage & Disadvantage Work

Because GM-controlled monsters and NPCs never roll dice, any condition, buff, or debuff granting a monster Advantage or Disadvantage exclusively inverts the player character's check:

* **Monster Advantage ➔ Character Disadvantage (2L20):** Character rolls at Disadvantage (e.g. monster has attack advantage ➔ character defends at Disadvantage).
* **Monster Disadvantage ➔ Character Advantage (2H20):** Character rolls at Advantage (e.g. monster is Weakened ➔ character defends at Advantage; monster has poison disadvantage ➔ character rolls poison affliction on monster with Advantage).

### 1.4 Exploding Dice

All attribute derived die rolls explode (i.e. all d4, d6, d8, d10, and d12) rolls that derive from attributes explode. This also includes Damage, Armor, Healing, and all d👣 movement rolls (both Jumps and Running/Sprinting). If the die rolls its maximum face, reroll that die and add the new result. If the reroll is also the max face, roll again, keep adding. Thus an exploding d6 “could” roll 6, 6, 6, 6, 4 for a total of 28.

#### Universal Explosion Rule for Attribute Rolls

* **Only Attribute Dice Explode:** In any Ability Roll (2H20 + d(Atr) or 1d20 + d(Atr)), ONLY the Attribute derived die explodes on its maximum face (e.g. rolling 6 on a d6). The d20 NEVER explodes (a 20 on a d20 is a Tremendous roll, not an exploding die).
* **Universal Explosion Directive:** ALL Attribute-based rolls (whether an Ability roll or a d(Atr) roll) explode on maximum face unless specifically stated otherwise. This includes Damage, Armor, Healing, and also Jumps (jump d👣) = all explode!

### 1.5 Tremendous 🌟 & Critical 💀

* **Tremendous (T)🌟:** Natural 20 on any ability roll’s d20. Triggers a spectacular narrative triumph or tactical windfall determined by the GM (bonus damage, weapon disarm, free repositioning).
* **Critical (C)💀:** Natural 1 on any ability roll’s d20. Triggers a notable complication or tactical setback (jammed weapon, loss of footing, dropped guard).
* All Ability Roll results stand as success or failure regardless of Tremendous or Critical effects. A Tremendous or Critical does not override the numbers — a natural 20 does not guarantee success, and a natural 1 does not guarantee failure. The roll result still determines success or failure. However, Tremendous rolls trigger beneficial narrative effects , and Critical rolls trigger detrimental narrative effects , as determined by the GM.
* **No Auto-Pass/Fail:** Natural 20 and Natural 1 do not override raw math; success or failure is determined EXCLUSIVELY by the total score vs. Dif and is not influenced by T🌟 or C💀 flags.

#### Double / Triple Results

* **Double Tremendous 🌟🌟 →** Two natural 20s in one roll (1 in 400 chance) = very powerful narrative effect.
* **Triple Tremendous 🌟🌟🌟 →** Three natural 20s in one roll (1 in 8,000 chance) = astoundingly powerful narrative effect.
* **Double Critical 💀💀 →** Two natural 1s = very significant negative effect.
* **Triple Critical 💀💀💀 →** Three natural 1s = catastrophic negative effect.

#### Mixed Rolls (T🌟 & C💀)

* **T/C Cancelation:** Tremendous 🌟 and Criticals 💀 cancel on a 1 for 1 basis.

#### 1.5.1 Tremendous & Critical Examples

🌟 Tremendous

* **Tremendous Attack ⚔️ —** Your spear thrust impales your target and carries through into the foe directly behind them (roll Dmg on both).
* **Tremendous Defense 🛡️ —** A perfectly timed parry twists the attacker’s weapon from their grip — it clatters d4 squares away.
* **Tremendous Skill Check 🎓 —** Your climbing grip is flawless; not only do you succeed, but you also set a safe line that lets allies climb at Adv for the rest of the Enc.

💀 Critical

* **Critical Attack ⚔️ —** Your greatsword lodges in the enemy’s armor — you deal no Dmg this rnd and must use a P action next turn to wrench it free.
* **Critical Defense —** A mistimed Dodge 🏃 sends you sprawling into the path of another threat — you go prone, and incoming Dmg is doubled.
* **Critical Skill Check 🎓 —** Your attempt to sneak trips a noisy latch; you’re exposed, and nearby monsters gain +2 Nish 🚩 this rnd.

🌟🌟 Double Tremendous (Two Natural 20s)

* **Double Tremendous Attack ⚔️ —** A warhammer strike obliterates your target outright, then ricochets with supernatural force into 1d4 nearby enemies for max Dmg before returning to your hand.
* **Double Tremendous Defense —** Your block not only deflects the blow but rebounds it — the attacker suffers their own full Dmg, and you may immediately reposition 3 squares and gain +3 Block Cap for the rest of the Enc.
* **Double Tremendous Skill Check 🎓 —** Your persuasion is so compelling that the guard doesn’t just let you through — he convinces his comrades to fight at your side for the rest of the Enc and remains your lifelong ally.

💀💀 Double Critical (Two Natural 1s)

* **Double Critical Attack ⚔️ —** Your bowstring snaps and the recoil slices your hand; you take 1 Wnd 🩸 and cannot use Rng attacks for the remainder of the Enc.
* **Double Critical Defense —** Your shield arm gives way under the impact; your shield is shattered, and you suffer the attack’s full Dmg ×2.
* **Double Critical Skill Check 🎓 —** Attempting to disarm a trap, you trigger it catastrophically — not only does it explode for full effect, but it also sets off a chain reaction that brings half the ceiling down (everyone Dodge 🏃^15 or suffer 12 Dmg vs AR 🧥).

**🌟🌟🌟 Triple Tremendous (Three Natural 20s —** rare!)

* **Triple Tremendous Attack ⚔️ —** Reality bends around your strike — your attack slays your foe, arcs lightning to every enemy in sight for triple full Dmg, and grants you and your allies +1 Luck 🍀 immediately.
* **Triple Tremendous Defense —** You intercept the blow in godlike fashion; not only are you unharmed, but you absorb the force and unleash it back as an auto-hit against all adjacent enemies equal to your max Dmg — and it slays the opponent that attacked you.
* **Triple Tremendous Skill Check 🎓 —** Your leap clears the chasm so perfectly that time seems to freeze; your allies may cross freely this Rnd as though flying, and all of you may cross this chasm at any time without fail.

**💀💀💀 Triple Critical (Three Natural 1s —** catastrophic!)

* **Triple Critical Attack ⚔️ —** Your magical weapon catastrophically fails — it shatters beyond repair, and you injure yourself for half your Vit ❤️ in Wnd 🩸s.
* **Triple Critical Defense —** You not only fail to defend but stumble directly into the most dangerous part of the attack — take double Dmg and suffer an Affliction (GM 🔎 choice: Stunned, Weakened, Immobilized, etc.), as a pit opens beneath you and you fall 20 feet.
* **Triple Critical Skill Check 🎓 —** The ritual backfires explosively — you not only fail but unleash a magical surge that Afflicts allies nearby with random conditions and causes the ceiling to collapse on all for 14 Dmg, save vs Dif 18 for half.

### 1.6 Reading the Rules: Emojis & Notation

Emojis 🙂 are used in SupaFlex for rapid identification of key stats and words. They are used in several ways and formats:

* Emojis 🙂 “may” come BEFORE their key word(s) in titles and headings ONLY.
* Otherwise, Emojis 🙂 follow their key word(s) in nearly every case.
* The five Attribute✅ (Atr) emojis can be used both following their key words as in: Magic ✨, Might 💪, Mind 👁️, Motion 🏃, Moxie 🫀, but also following any ability as an indicator as to which of the five attributes that ability is derived from (e.g., “Stealth 🏃” is the preferred shorthand for “Stealth roll using Motion 🏃”).
* **Bolt ⚡:** The ⚡ emoji strictly represents a Bolt (5 accumulated Sparks). Individual charges are called Sparks or a Spark. When a hero accumulates 5 sparks, they have a Bolt ⚡, which can power any ability with the usage of “1-⚡”.

## Part 2 · Your Hero

**Who your hero is:** attributes, resources, abilities, gear, and the step-by-step creation process.

### 2.1 Attributes ✅

Each character begins with five core Attributes (Atr✅). Each Attribute is expressed as a rating: 4, 6, 8, 10, or 12. These represent a character’s natural ability in that category.

* **Magic ✨ (Mag) —** Super-natural abilities. Governs spellcasting, channeling Powers 🔥.
* **Might 💪 (Mgt) —** Brute force, melee weapons Atk ⚔️ and Dmg, armor size (Strength Saves), block Def, shields, physical strength, lifting
* **Mind 👁️ (Mnd) —** Logic, knowledge, shot weapons Atk ⚔️ and Dmg, awareness (Mental Saves), intelligence, personality, wit, charm, persuade, reason, cunning, and mental presence. Governs insight 👁️, deception, intimidation, persuasion
* **Motion 🏃 (Mot) —** Agility, Nish 🚩, movement, hurled weapons Atk ⚔️ and Dmg (Reflex Saves), dodge, athletics, dexterity, balance, reflexes, sneaking, acrobatics
* **Moxie 🫀 (Mox) —** Stamina, grit, vitality, and stamina. Governs Vit ❤️, death checks, unconscious checks, and most bodily resistance checks

**How Attributes Work:**

* **Attribute ratings are rolled in derived Ability Rolls:** #d20 + d(Atr) + Bonus vs Dif.
* Attribute ratings start at **one 4, two 6, two 8** across the five attributes at Level 1, improving through tier-gated AP advancement up to a hard maximum array of **one 6, one 8, one 10, two 12** at Level 100+.
* Attribute derived checks and rolls always use the rating tied to the relevant Attribute.
* **Balance Note:** All Attributes are equally important. Mgt 💪 is not inherently better than Mnd 👁️, nor Mot 🏃 better than Mag ✨ or Mox 🫀. Players succeed by leveraging their strongest ratings in creative ways.

#### ✅ Atr Tags

**All abilities and items show their governing Atr:** Might 💪, Motion 🏃, Mind 👁️, Magic ✨, or Moxie 🫀 that they are derived from. Note: Atr-derived rolls ALWAYS explode.

Attribute shorthand in SupaFlex follows a clean, five-part notation standard across powers, exotics, items, and all other rules. This makes it immediately clear whether you are making an ability roll, rolling against a target, rolling an attribute die, or using your flat attribute score.

### 2.2 Vitality ❤️, Luck 🍀 & Focus 🎯

Vitality ❤️ is your health pool. Starting Vit is set in §2.5 (Step 4); damage and dying are covered in §4.6–4.7.

#### Luck 🍀

* Spend 1 Luck 🍀 to **reroll any entire, in-game, roll**. A roll can ONLY be lucked once.
* **Luck Twist:** Spend 1 🍀 to grant an instant tactical boost: +1 Target, +1d (Die Type), +1 Range Band, or +1 effect such as Push/Move square.
* Activate Any Power (1/Enc): Once per encounter, spend 1 Luck 🍀 to activate any known Power 🔥 (including Exotic 🧿 Powers 🔥), regardless of its Usage (1-🍀, 1-⚡, 1-Enc, 2-Enc, 3-Enc, or 1-Rnd).

#### Focus 🎯

**Focus Roll:**

* **Use —** Once per Ability roll (and Dmg, AR, Healing), after seeing the initial roll result, roll your current Focus Die and add the result directly to your total. Focus rolls explode and stack with EVERYTHING.
* **Step-Down Rule —** Focus Die steps down by -1d whenever the Focus roll is neither its maximum face nor a 1 (d12 ➔ d10 ➔ d8 ➔ d6 ➔ d4 ➔ Exhausted). For example, on a d6, the die steps down on a roll of 2, 3, 4, or 5, but stays at d6 on a 1 or 6.
* **Usage Limit —** Limit 1 Focus spend per roll.

**Refilling Focus:**

* **Start of Combat —** Step up +1d at the beginning of an encounter.
* **End of Combat —** Step up +1d upon resolving an encounter.
* **Short Rest —** Step up +1d when taking a short rest opportunity between combats, limited to one use between combat encounters.
* **Full Round Action (Flooding) —** Spend an entire combat round taking no other actions to "Flood" (+1d step up).
* **Full Rest —** A full night's sleep completely restores your Focus Die to its maximum ceiling size.

### 2.3 Abilities & Paths 🧭

* **Elements 🌟 = All Abilities🧠 + All Gear ⚙️:**
* **Abilities🧠:** Unlocked via AP and packaged into Paths 🧭. Subdivided into Skill 🎓, Powers 🔥, and Traits 🧬.

**Paths 🧭 vs. Kits 📦:**

* **Paths 🧭:** Character ability suites (Race Paths, Class Paths, Discipline Paths, Specialization Paths, Innate Path, Universal Path). Unlocked via AP and character creation.
* **Kits 📦:** Gear ⚙️ bundles (e.g. Field Repair Kit, Trauma Kit, Survival Kit). Purchased with Gold / Silver (g/s)

#### The Four Foundational Paths

* **Starting & Foundational Paths 🧭:** Every character starts with two selected and Learned Paths (a Race Path and a Class Path) plus two free Paths (the Innate Path and the Universal Path):
  * **Innate Path 🧭: The path of base abilities that ALL characters have (including Language Common for free Trait [0 AP], plus 1 AP learnable skills like Nish 🚩, Resist Death 🫀, and Resist Unconsciousness 🫀, and natural/improvised proficiencies).**
  * **Race Path 🧭:** Defines species traits, biology, and innate racial rules. Can grant abilities costing 1 AP, Free (0 AP), or both.
  * **Class Path 🧭:** Defines starting archetype, core skill set options, proficient weapons, armor, and starting powers. Can grant abilities costing 1 AP, Free (0 AP), or both.
  * **Universal Path 🧭:** An open, permanent global pool of high-impact abilities. Universal Path abilities may be learned by ANY character for 3 AP (the typical Out-of-Path cost) and do NOT require GM approval.
* **In-Path Abilities —** Abilities in your active Paths cost 1 AP to learn (if you meet requirements).
* **Out-of-Path Surcharge —** Learning an ability outside your active Paths is Out of Path and costs +2AP (i.e. 3 AP) AND requires GM approval.
* **Learning Bonus Paths:** Beyond foundational paths, additional Paths may be learned for 4 AP WITH GM Approval (e.g. unlocking a specialty or high level dual class).

### 2.4 Gear ⚙️

The complete SupaFlex game system is structured around the Trinity of Mechanics:

* **1. Ownership (What you Know & Possess):** Intangible character capabilities (Paths 🧭) are unlocked with Adventure Points (AP)🧩; tangible Gear ⚙️ is purchased with Currency (Gold / Silver) or found as treasure.
* **2. Execution (What you Do in Combat):** Follows the Action Economy (1 Attack [A] + 1 Move [M] + 1 Partial [P] + Unlimited Free [F]).
* **3. Capabilities (What you Learn & Wield):** Tactical gear powers (Exotics 🧿 Powers 🔥) cost exactly 1 AP to learn and are rooted physically in owned host gear chassis and installed mods.

| **⚙️ GEAR (Possessions)** | Gold / Silver (g/s) or Treasure | • Supplies 🎒 • Weapons ⚔️ • Armor 🧥 • Shields 🛡️ • Kits 📦 | Tangible physical equipment owned in inventory. Gear costs g/s or is found as treasure. |
| **🧿 EXOTICS (Gear with Mods)** | Gold / Silver (g/s) or Treasure | • Inherent Chassis Mods 📦 • Installed Aftermarket Mods 🔌 | Purchasable/Treasure equipment possessing tactical combat abilities. Rooted in physical gear chassis. |

| **Exotic 🧿 Powers 🔥** | Adventure Points (AP) | Always belong to an Exotic’s🧿 Mod 🔌 | Functions nearly identical to regular Powers 🔥 but is gear derived |

**⚡ Quick Reference:** Gear & Equipment Kits

* **Gear ⚙️ —** The universal master category for all physical items, hardware, and equipment, including Artifacts 🔮.
* **Standard Gear ⚙️ —** Gear ⚙️ with no Exotic 🧿 mods (priced in g/s).
* **Kits 📦 —** Pre-packaged sets of Gear ⚙️ (Dungeoneering, Infiltration, Field Medic, Survival, etc.) for a flat Gold/Silver (g/s) price.
* **No Weight or Encumbrance —** SupaFlex uses no weight values, bulky tracking, or encumbrance movement penalties for any gear ⚙️.

### 2.5 Creating Your Hero, Step by Step

Follow these steps in order. Each step points to the section with the full rules.

#### Step 1 · Concept

**Concept First:** Any idea works — punk knight, techno-bard, gargoyle hero, soda rogue, etc.

#### Step 2 · Paths

Choose a Race Path 🧭 and a Class Path 🧭. You also automatically have the Innate Path 🧭 and access to the Universal Path 🧭. See §2.3 for what Paths grant and what learning costs.

#### Step 3 · Attributes

Assign **one 4, two 6, two 8** across Magic ✨, Might 💪, Mind 👁️, Motion 🏃, and Moxie 🫀.

#### Step 4 · Vitality

* **Vit ❤️:** 10 + 1d(🫀) (e.g., Moxie 8 = 10+1d8 = 11 to 18).
* Only during creation, a 1d(🫀) roll of 1 may be re-rolled one time, in hopes of a better result.

#### Step 5 · Focus

Character starts with a Focus Die sized at **d4** with purchasable upgrades gated by level, (see Advancement).

#### Step 6 · Spend 8 Adventure Points

Your character is 1st Level ⭐ (record 1 for Level ⭐) and begins with **8 Starting AP 🧩** to shape their initial capabilities. In-Path elements cost 1 AP (2 AP if requirements are not met), and do not require GM approval.

A standard, balanced character build typically spends these 8 AP across the following areas:

* 🎓 1 in Path Skill Set (2 AP) if one exists.
* ⚔️ 1 in-path Weapon (1 AP) if one exists.
* 🧥 1 in-path Armor (1 AP) if one exists.
* 🔥 3 in-path Powers (3 AP)
* Learn 3 starting Powers 🔥. A recommended approach is taking one each of these:
  * 1 Act A Power 🔥 usable at least 1-Enc for encounter impact.
  * 1 Act P Power 🔥 that you can turn on 1-Rnd so it can be on anytime you are not otherwise using a P action.
  * 1 Usage 1-⚡ Power 🔥 for that big WOW factor.

🧩 1 Flexible AP (1 AP)

Spend your remaining 1 Starting AP 🧩 on a flexible upgrade of your choice, such as:

* A Shield 🛡️ (1 AP)
* A 2nd Weapon ⚔️ (1 AP)
* Nish 🚩 Skill (1 AP)
* Resist Death 🫀 or Resist Unconsciousness 🫀 Skill (1 AP)
* +2 Max Vit ❤️ (1 AP)

* **Hgt/Wgt/Age:** Your height, weight, and age.

## Part 3 · Playing the Game

**How play flows outside of combat:** time, skills, rest, and dangers.

### 3.1 Time: Sessions, Days, Encounters & Rounds

#### 📅 Days

* A new Day📅 begins each new character day (after sleep).
* When characters start their new Day📅, they are in a new Day📅 AND a new Roleplaying Enc.

#### 🎲 Encounters

* The game’s Days📅 are broken into Encs (Encounters).
* An Enc is both the mode the game enters when the GM calls for first Nish 🚩 and the game proceeds rnd by rnd (often during combat or other intense timing events), AND the period between such combat encounters is ALSO an encounter - a roleplaying Enc.
* **Example sequence:**
  * **Enc 1 (Roleplaying):** The party meets in a tavern with heavy roleplaying.
  * **Enc 2 (Combat):** A fight breaks out and the game switches into Rnd mode.
  * **Enc 3 (Roleplaying):** After the fight, the party searches opponents, rests, visits an inn, and sleeps.
* Each of these is classified as an “Enc” in SupaFlex. This approach means an Enc can either be a combat Enc or a roleplaying Enc.
* This classification defines (1-Enc, 2-Enc, 3-Enc) ability usage.
* **Example:** A healer who can heal 2-Enc may heal 2 times per Enc (2 in each combat encounter AND 2 in each roleplaying encounter).
* In the sequence above, they could heal 6 times in total (2 each in the 3 Encounters).

### 3.2 Using Skills 🎓

**Rolls:**

* **Unskilled Checks —** Unknown skills (including weapons, armor, and shields) may be attempted as an Unskilled Ability check (1d20 + d(Atr)).
* **Skilled Checks —** Known skills, or those approved by the GM as matching a Skill Set, use a Skilled Ability check (2H20 + d(Atr)).
* **Powers & Exotics —** All Powers 🔥 and Exotics 🧿 are ALWAYS considered Skilled.
* **Skill Action Cost:** Skills 🎓 default to a Partial (P) action unless an ability or GM specifies Free (F). GM-initiated awareness or perception checks are always Free (F).

#### 🎓 Skills

* **Derived:** Attributes are NEVER rolled directly as skilled or unskilled rolls. There is no rolling of (Magic ✨, Might 💪, Mind 👁️, Motion 🏃, Moxie 🫀) directly. Rather, anytime an ability roll or d(Atr) is indicated, the player is rolling for a “Skill 🎓” or Ability🧠 that is DERIVED FROM that attribute. Even when a character tries to lift a weight and the effect indicates “Roll 💪”, the player is actually rolling a “Strength” or “Lifting” skill, they are NOT rolling Might directly. This is an important distinction. This includes Nish 🚩, Saves ✨, Resistances ✨, all Vit ❤️, weapons ⚔️, all Armor 🧥, all skills 🎓, all uses of Might 💪, Motion 🏃, Mind 👁️, Magic ✨, and Moxie 🫀, and everything else.
* Skills (weapons ⚔️, Armor 🧥, Shield 🛡️, skills 🎓) are all unskilled unless explicitly learned with AP 🧩 (thus becoming skilled) or the GM determines the skill is part of a known skill set 🎓.
* **Anything learned via AP 🧩 is skilled —** including a weapon ⚔️, Armor 🧥, Power 🔥, skill 🎓, or Skill Set 🎓, etc.
* When a skill 🎓 is rolled, its die type or value is derived from its listed or GM selected Atr✅ (e.g., Acrobatics 🏃 = a 2H20 + d🏃 derived check).
* ALL unknown skills 🎓 may be attempted unskilled. This includes Stealth, climbing, identifying an Artifact 🔮, using any weapon, armor, shield, etc.

#### 🎓 Skill Negotiation

Skills 🎓 are intentionally open-ended. When a player uses a skill:

* The player states what they want to achieve.
* The GM 🔎 sets a Dif (or proposes a different framing).
* **The player may negotiate —** proposing tools, narrative creativity, staged attempts, or background justification to alter the Dif.
* If both sides agree, the player rolls the ability check (Skilled = 2H20, Unskilled = 1d20).

**This creates flexibility:** players may attempt anything, limited only by GM approval.Examples:

* **Example:** “I’ll climb in two stages with rope and spikes at half MR 👣.” → GM may lower Dif and allow a backup roll on failure.
* **Example:** “I use my healing 🎓 with detailed triage, splints, and temple background.” → GM may shift a basic stabilize roll into a chance to restore Vit ❤️.
* **Example:** “I add narrative detail to my Stealth 🏃.” → GM may set a lower Dif.

#### 🎓 Character Assists

* When one or more characters help another (e.g. Character A is picking a lock and Character B is helping). The rolling character now has an Advantage at the GM’s discretion.

#### 🎓 Skills vs Powers

* Powers 🔥 are concrete, mechanical, and balanced with action and usage limits and have rules of their own called “Effects”.
* Skills 🎓 are flexible, narrative tools that are a one roll success or failure.
* Skills may alter Dif, reduce damage, grant a minor one-off bonus, or open a path forward.
* **Skills should not overshadow Powers 🔥 —** they are meant to be quick, creative, situational, and dynamic.

#### 🎓 Skill Sets

* A Skill Set 🎓 is a logical collection of related skills.
* Learning an In-Path Skill Set 🎓 costs **2 AP 🧩** (Out-of-Path costs **4 AP 🧩** with GM Approval) and makes all skills 🎓 in that set skilled.
* **Skill Sets 🎓 are not exhaustive lists —** the GM may approve additional related skills not listed upon player request. It is more important for the GM to consider the "spirit" of the Skill Set 🎓 than the limited list.

### 3.3 Rest & Recovery 💤

After a combat encounter, the roleplaying encounter will often begin with post-combat rest.

* **Short Rest (Post-Combat / Roleplaying Enc Rest):** A brief rest after battle restores d4 Vit ❤️ (limit 1 short rest per combat encounter) and steps up your Focus Die 🎯 by +1d.
* **Full Rest (Sleep / New Day 📅):** A full night's sleep restores 2d4 Vit ❤️ and completely restores your Focus Die 🎯 to its maximum size.
* **First Aid & Healing —** Medical supplies, bandaging, and healing skills stop active bleeding.
* **Magical Healing —** Will stop active bleeding and heal as per the power or item’s rules.

### 3.4 Hazards & Afflictions 💀

#### 🌋 Hazard 💀

* A **Hazard** is an environmental feature, battlefield obstacle, dangerous terrain situation, or trap that can either initiate an Affliction, trigger damage, and/or a Resistance roll.
* **Examples:** Pit traps, walls of fire, pools of lava, collapsing ceilings, toxic gas clouds, slippery ice, or very rough ground (trip hazard).
* **Resolution:** Entering, crossing, or being forced into a hazard triggers an immediate roll (e.g. Save 🫀 or Poisoned, Save 🏃 or fall into pit, Save ✨ or 2d6 Dmg, Def & AR roll, etc.).

#### Affliction 💀

* An **Affliction** is an ongoing physical, physiological, mental, or magical ailment or condition suffered by a target (character or monster). The afflicted target gets to roll versus the affliction immediately, and if failed, each Nish 🚩 to try and recover or end the affliction.
* **Afflictions Examples:** Poison, Disease, Death (via Death Checks), Burning, Weakness, Suffocation, Held, Immobilized, Stunned, Paralyzed, Frozen, Blinded, etc.
* **Character Affliction Recovery:** When a character suffers from an affliction, they immediately get to make the indicated recovery roll (e.g. 🫀^20, Resist Death^-Vit+5, 💪^💪, etc.) as an Act(F) upon infliction, and on each of the character's Nishes 🚩 until the character succeeds or dies.
* **Monster Affliction Recovery:** Because only players roll dice in SupaFlex, monsters NEVER roll dice. When a monster is afflicted, the character who inflicted it rolls the designated check (e.g. 💪^💪 or 👁️^🫀) immediately upon infliction, and on each of that character's Nishes 🚩 to see if the monster is still afflicted or breaks free.
* **Fatigue Wounds vs. Affliction Triggers:** If an Affliction triggers upon inflicting Wounds 🩸, Fatigue Wounds do NOT trigger the affliction (as fatigue bruising/tiring implies no skin piercing or toxin delivery). However, if the target's Armor (AR 🧥) fails to absorb all rolled Damage, the weapon has pierced flesh, and the affliction applies normally.

#### 📉 Weakness Affliction

**When a target suffers from the Weakness Affliction:**

* **1. Ability Rolls are Disadvantaged:** All of the target's ability rolls (Attack checks, Defense checks, Attribute checks, and Saves) are rolled at Disadvantage. If a monster is Weakened, any player character rolling against that monster rolls at Advantage per the Monster Inversion Doctrine.
* **2. Movement Rate Halved (Rounded Up):** The target's Movement Rate 👣 is halved, rounded up (e.g. 👣5 becomes 👣3, 👣3 becomes 👣2).
* **3. No Effect on Damage or Armor:** Weakened has NO effect on rolls or stats that cannot be disadvantaged, such as weapon Damage rolls (Dmg ) or Armor absorption rolls (AR 🧥).

#### 🎲 Every Affliction Allows a Roll

In SupaFlex, rarely does a target (friend, foe, or PC) simply have an unavoidable negative effect or damage applied passively without an active check, saving throw, or C or T trigger. Hostile effects, conditions, and area attacks should almost ALWAYS involve an active roll (e.g. Save ✨ or Dmg ✨, Save 🫀 or Poisoned, Def or Dmg d8).

##### 💀 Core Afflictions, Hazards & Status Conditions

Status conditions and Afflictions are binary, high-impact tactical states.

##### The Zero-Duration Rule

SupaFlex **RARELY** appends durations to afflictions. Recovery from afflictions is governed automatically by the core Affliction recovery rules (the player rolls Atr^Target Atr on their Nish to see if the foe breaks free, or rolls on their own Nish if afflicted).

##### Poison Hazard & Affliction Specification Standard

Unlike uniform conditions (Stunned, Weakened, Prone, Blinded), **Poison is a diverse category** with varied toxicity levels. All poison applications MUST state their tick rate or toxin type:

* **Ongoing damage rate:** Poisoned 1 Wnd/Rnd, Poisoned 2 Wnd/Rnd, or Poisoned d4 Wnds/Rnd.
* **Symptom-based toxin:** Poisoned (paralysis) or Poisoned (nausea).

## Part 4 · Combat ⚔️

**How a fight works, round by round:** initiative, actions, attacking, defending, damage, and dying.

### 4.1 A Combat Round at a Glance

* **Initiative (Nish 🚩):** Act in order from highest to lowest Nish. On ties, players act before monsters.
* **Action Pool on Your Turn:** You receive a fresh pool of 4 Actions every round:
  * **Act(A) Attack:** Make a weapon attack, invoke an offensive Power, or execute a martial maneuver.
  * **Act(M) Move:** Move up to your full Movement Rate in squares (MR 👣).You may freely pause and resume movement (move ➔ pause ➔ move). But you may NOT split MR 👣 on both sides of an Attack Action (NO move ➔ attack ➔ move). You MAY use your actions in any order such as M then A or A then M, with your one P and unlimited F interspersed as desired.
* **Act(P) Partial:** Utility action (with a free hand)—quaff a potion, swap weapons, reload crossbow/firearm, bandage a wound, or interact with the environment. (Requires a Move [M] action when using a two-handed weapon, dual wielding, or Weapon & Shield; see §5.2).
  * **Act(F) Free:** Unlimited minor actions and reactions—speech, dropping an item, Dodge, or executing a Block.
  * **Act(AM) Attack & Move:** Combine your Attack and Move to sprint (👣 + d👣 squares), execute a running charge, and other powerful options.

### 4.2 Initiative (Nish 🚩)

* **Nish 🚩:** Common abbreviation for Initiative.
* **Nish 🚩 Check:** PCs roll 🚩. The GM sequences the PC’s Nish rolls with the static Monster Nishes.
* Determine Nish 🚩 order. Proceed from highest to lowest Nish among All PCs and Monster. Each PC/Monster takes a turn on their Nish 🚩.
* A turn = AMPF or 1 Atk ⚔️ (A) + 1 Move (M) + 1 Partial (P) + Unlimited Free (F) actions.
* If afflicted on each Nish 🚩 roll a new Resistance checks vs the affliction (Stunned, Weakened, Immobilized, Poisoned, etc.).

### 4.3 Actions 🔷

Every combat round, each character receives an action allocation of **1 Attack (A)**, **1 Move (M)**, and **1 Partial (P)**, plus **Unlimited Free (F)** actions (within GM reason). These action channels are never interchangeable (you cannot trade an A or M for a P).

#### The 5 Strict Action Channels

| Action | Designation | Tactical Scope & Cost |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **AM** | Attack & Move | Combined action consuming both your 1 Attack (A) and 1 Move (M) allocations for the round. Leaves your 1 Partial (P) and unlimited Free (F) actions available. |
| **A** | Attack | Any weapon strike, martial attack, or offensive ability, consumes your 1 Attack allocation for the round. |
| **M** | Move | Any tactical movement (from 1 sq up to your full MR 👣), OR performing a utility task while using a two-handed weapon, dual wielding, or Weapon & Shield (§5.2). |
| **P** | Partial | Minor, preparatory, or interacting utility tasks with a free hand (drawing a blade, donning a shield, quaffing a potion, opening a door). Consumes your 1 Partial allocation. (Requires a Move [M] action if using a two-handed weapon, dual wielding, or Weapon & Shield). |
| **F** | Free | **Trivial, instantaneous reactions:** Defenses (Block 🛡️, Dodge 🏃, AR 🧥), saves, quick verbal communication, or dropping an item. Unlimited per round (at GM discretion). |

#### Tactical Movement & The Mobile Striker Clause

* **Standard Attack & Move Sequence:** If you do not use your (A) Action, you may freely pause and resume your Move (M) (move ➔ pause ➔ move). However, taking an Attack (A) action immediately concludes your turn's movement IF 1 or more MR has already been used; any unused Movement Rate (MR) is forfeit.
* **Running (Sprinting): Running requires an Attack & Move (AM) action and grants full ground movement of MR + d👣 squares (👣+d👣 sq). All d👣 rolls explode.**
* **Difficult Terrain:** Any tactical grid square (5x5 ft) designated as Difficult Terrain requires 2 or more Movement Rate 👣 points to enter, rather than the standard 1 point. The universal difficult terrain default, unless otherwise noted, is a 👣 cost of 2 per square. Severe obstructions like waist-deep mud or steep rubble can have a 3 or higher 👣 cost per sq.
* **Breaking Melee:** Moving more than the melee reach away from an opponent who has engaged you in melee while they have Nish in that round provokes an Opportunity Attack. See Opportunity Attacks.
* **Mobile Striker Clause:** Specialized Move (M) or Partial (P) Powers 🔥 or items can explicitly grant move-Atk-move ("You may split your movement before and after this attack"), providing tactical identity for mobility builds.
* **Jumping Actions:** Standing Jump is d👣 squares as a Move (M) action. Running Jump is running up to MR 👣 on the ground plus a d👣 jump as an Attack & Move (AM) action. All d👣 rolls explode (both Jumps and Running).

**Examples of Partial (P) Actions (or a Move [M] action when using a two-handed weapon, dual wielding, or Weapon & Shield):**

* Open a door.
* Draw weapon ⚔️
* Don shield 🛡️
* Pick up an item.
* Stand from prone
* Drink or Eat
* A brief talk

**Examples of Free (F) Actions:**

* All Def rolls (Block 🛡️, Dodge 🏃, AR 🧥)
* Any Save or Resistance
* GM-requested roll (e.g., GM asks for Awareness 👁️)
* Face change
* A few words
* Sheath weapon ⚔️
* Un-don (put away) a shield 🛡️
* Drop an item
* Kneel
* Dive prone
* Close a door

#### 1 Round Action Examples

* **Charge and Strike →** Move (M) across the battlefield, Attack (A) with your longsword, and Partial (P) to draw a shield mid-swing.
* **Shoot and Relocate →** Attack (A) with a bow, Move (M) behind cover, and Partial (P) to drop prone.
* **Dagger Flurry →** Attack (A) twice using a multi-attack (if your Might allows), Move (M) to shift one square, and Partial (P) to kick a chair into an opponent’s path (but NOT at an opponent which would be an illegal 2nd Attack (A) action), then finish your Move up to max MR.
* **Defensive Prep & Punch →** A hands free character: Move (M) into position, Partial (P) to don a shield, and Attack (A) with a punch (Brawl) as there isn’t another Partial (P) action to also draw out a weapon.
* **Potion Break →** Attack (A) with a mace, Partial (P) to quaff a healing potion, and Move (M) toward safety.
* **Battlefield Control →** Move (M) to a doorway, Free (F) to slam it shut, and Attack (A) to strike an enemy adjacent.
* **Quick Cast →** Attack (A) with a spell (firebolt), Move (M) three squares back, and Partial (P) to activate a magical trinket.
* **Opportunistic Fighter →** Attack (A) with dual weapons, Move (M) around the enemy to flank, and Free (F) to shout a warning to allies.
* **Agile Scout →** Move (M) your full MR across rough terrain, Partial (P) to make an awareness check to potentially see a hidden foe, and Attack (A) with a hurled dagger.
* **Show of Flair →** Attack (A) with a rapier, Free (F) to bow dramatically (Flair), Move (M) into cover, and Free (F) to verbally taunt your opponent.
* **Shoot and Scoot →** Attack (A) with a bow, Move (M) down the hallway, Free (F) to shut a door behind you.

### 4.4 Attacking

* **Choose Target & Weapon ⚔️ —** Action is (A).
* **Roll to Hit:** if Skilled, roll 2H20 + d(Atr) + Bonus vs Monster Def (5 to 30). If Unskilled, roll 1d20.
* d(Atr) = the weapon’s governing Atr (melee 💪, hurled 🏃, or shot 👁️). Remember d(Atr) explodes.
* Tremendous 🌟 (20 on d20). Critical 💀 (1 on d20).
* **On Success →** Roll Dmg: plus any bonus. Unless using the multi-attack rules Dmg will be the same d(Atr) at the Atk. Dmg is exploding.
* **Monster Armor 🧥:** Subtract monster’s Armor 🧥 value (0 to 3, rarely 4+) to get Wnds caused.
* **Apply Wnd 🩸:** Add the wounds to any existing and record it next to the monster. Once total Wounds reach or exceed the monster’s Vit ❤️, the monster dies.

#### Opportunity Attacks

* **Trigger Requirements —** Previously engaged in MELEE, currently have Initiative (Nish 🚩), holding a melee weapon (or using Brawl) and monster moves out of melee range OR performs a distracting action (casts a spell, quaffs a potion, inspects an item).
* **Attack Resolution —** Immediate, Free (F) BASIC reaction attack (no Focus 🎯, Luck 🍀, Powers 🔥, or Exotics 🧿 allowed).

### 4.5 Defending: Dodge, Block & Resist

When a monster attacks you, choose **one (and only one)** active defense:

| 🛡️ Tactical Defense | Scope & Weapon Requirements | Success (Beats Atk) | Failure (Hit Connects) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **🏃 DODGE** | Any incoming Attack (Melee, Ranged, AoE, or Spells). | 0 Damage (Clean evasion; attack completely avoided). | Take Hit ➔ Proceed to Armor Roll (AR 🧥). |
| **🛡️ BLOCK with MELEE** | Melee Attacks ONLY Incoming Dmg is at or below the Weapon’s Max Block. | 0 Damage (Clean deflection; attack completely blocked). | Take Hit ➔ Proceed to Armor Roll (AR 🧥). |
| **🛡️ BLOCK with SHIELD** | Any Dodgeable Attack. Incoming Dmg is at or below the Shield’s Max Block. | 0 Damage (Clean deflection; attack completely blocked). | Take Hit ➔ Proceed to Armor Roll (AR 🧥). |
| **RESIST/SAVE** | If attack/ability is NOT dodgeable nor blockable. | Varies, default is 0 Damage, but can be save for ½ Dmg, etc. | RARELY does AR 🧥 apply. Typically upon failure the Affliction applies including any Dmg as direct Wnds. |

**🧥 Damage Mitigation:** Rolled Armor (AR 🧥) is subtracted from incoming Damage. Any remaining Damage becomes Wounds (Wnds), subtracted from Vitality ❤️.

**Dodge 🏃:**

* Works against **any** incoming attack (Melee, Shot, Hurled, and Dodgeable Powers).
* **Formula:** Roll 2H20 + d🏃 + Bonus (Skilled) or 1d20 + d🏃 + Bonus (Unskilled) against the Monster's Attack score (typically 5 to 30).
* **Result:** Beats/Ties monster Attack = completely avoid the attack (0 damage). Fails = attack hits; immediately roll Armor AR 🧥 to reduce damage into Wounds (Wnds).

**Block 🛡️ (⚔️ Weapon or 🛡️ Shield):**

* **Scope & Cap:** Requires a held melee weapon (against melee attacks only) or a drawn shield (against any weapon attack and GM-approved blockable abilities). You may only Block when the incoming Dmg is at or below your weapon's or shield's Max Block 🛡️ rating.
* **Formula:** Roll 2H20 + d💪 + Bonus (Skilled) or 1d20 + d💪 + Bonus (Unskilled) against the Monster's Attack score (5 to 30).
* **Result:** Beats monster Attack = completely negates all damage (0 damage). Fails = hit connects; immediately roll Armor AR 🧥 to reduce damage to Wnds.

**Resist/Saves:**

* **Scope:** Used against powers, curses, breath weapons, explosions, poisons, and other effects that cannot be Dodged or Blocked.
* **Formula & Governing Attribute:** Roll 2H20 + d(Atr) + Bonus (Skilled) or 1d20 + d(Atr) + Bonus (Unskilled) using the Attribute matched to the threat: Moxie 🫀 (most common: poison, disease, exhaustion, bodily endurance), Magic ✨ (arcane effects), Might 💪 (physical force), Mind 👁️ (mental or charm effects), or Motion 🏃 (area blasts and explosions).

**Master Invariant:** No attack may be both Blocked and Dodged. However, Armor AR 🧥 ALWAYS rolls if your Dodge or Block fails!

### 4.6 Damage, Armor & Wounds

* **Damage Resolution:** Incoming Damage -AR 🧥 = Wounds (Wnds) which is the amount of Vitality ❤️ (Vit)❤️ Lost.
* All d(Atr) and AR 🧥 explode.
* **If Defense Fails →** Player Armor AR 🧥: Roll armor die (d4 to d12) (exploding rules apply) and subtract result from Monster Dmg to get actual Wnds 🩸.
* **FATIGUE (Ftg) Wnds 🩸:** Monsters inflict Fatigue Wnds 🩸 by default based on Dmg (ten's digit + 1: Monster Dmg 1–9 = 1Ftg 🩸, 10–19 = 2Ftg 🩸, 20–29 = 3Ftg 🩸, etc.; huge/giant monsters or elites may modify this). If your AR 🧥 results in less Wnds 🩸 than this Fatigue value, the character suffers the Fatigue Wnds 🩸 instead. Fatigue Wnds 🩸 do NOT trigger Afflictions if the AR 🧥 roll would have stopped ALL of the Dmg.
* **Apply Wnd 🩸:** Add to your total Wnd 🩸.

### 4.7 Down & Dying

* **Zero or Negative Vit Affliction Order & Unified Triggers: Once a character’s Current Vit ❤️ is 0 or below, three afflictions/checks apply in strict order: 1. Bleeding, 2. Death Check, 3. Unconscious Check. All three share unified triggers: (1) upon reaching Vit 0 or below, (2) anytime more Wnds 🩸 are received while at Vit 0 or below, and (3) on each of the character’s Nishes 🚩 starting next round until stabilized (Vit above 0). The one exception is that Bleeding does not apply upon the character’s first time entering Vit 0 or below, and Bleeding is not triggered by more Wnds 🩸 received.**
* **1. Bleeding (-1 Vit): On each of the character’s Nishes 🚩 starting the round after reaching Vit 0 or below (never on initial entry into Vit 0 or below, and never when receiving more Wnds 🩸), the character suffers +1 Wound 🩸 (-1 Vit ❤️) from arterial bleeding/power loss until receiving First Aid, triage, or magical healing.**
* **2. Death Check (Resist Death 🫀): At Vit 0 or below, roll Resist Death 🫀 as a skill check at 🫀^-Vit+5 (e.g., at Vit 0 it is 🫀^5; at Vit -8 it is 🫀^13). Failing a Death Check results in death.**
* **3. Unconscious Check (Resist Unconsciousness 🫀): At Vit 0 or below, roll Resist Unconsciousness 🫀 as a skill check at 🫀^-Vit+10 (e.g., at Vit 0 it is 🫀^10; at Vit -8 it is 🫀^18). Failing renders the character unconscious (collapses Prone and cannot take actions).**

### 4.8 Range & Area 🎯

Powers 🔥 and Weapons ⚔️ eliminate fiddly footage counting in favor of 8 standard Tactical Range Bands:

#### The 8 Tactical Range Bands

| **Self** | Caster / user only | Personal buffs, stances, and defensive auras. |
| **Touch** | Direct physical contact | Medical triage, infusions, and close-contact touch powers. |

| **Medium** | Up to 12 sq | Standard ranged weapons (shortbows, pistols) and mid-range spells. |
| **Long** | Up to 24 sq | Military bows, rifles, and long-range tactical artillery. |

#### 📐 AoE

When abilities affect multiple squares, they always use clean, grid-friendly shapes:

| Format | Geometry & Measurement Standard | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **AoE [#]r** | **Radius Burst:** Centered on a designated sq, affecting that sq AND all squares within #sq radius. | 2r (burst extending 2 squares in all directions from target sq). (This is a circle inscribed in a 5x5 grid) |
| **[#]x[#]** | **Rectangular Footprint:** Stated directly as width × length in squares. | 3x6 (rectangular footprint covering 3 squares wide by 6 squares long). |

**📌 Important —** Strict Prohibition of Cones: Traditional tabletop "cones" are strictly prohibited in SupaFlex to eliminate ambiguous grid templates, table arguments, and diagonal-edge disputes. All spread or sweeping effects are cleanly represented as rectangular footprints or #r such as 1r (e.g., a fiery breath or shotgun spray is standardized as a 2x6, 1x4, etc. rectangles).

### 4.9 Summoned & Hired Allies 🤖

Any entity created, summoned, fabricated, or purchased that can initiate combat or be targeted by attacks (e.g. companion animals, combat drones, conjured elementals, androids, homunculi) operates under **PC Atr Inheritance**:

* **Automatic Attribute Scaling (How Allies Use Your Stats):**
* **Base Rule:** Summoned/Crate/Hired/Companion/etc. entities inherit the player character's current attribute ratings as its base Atr AND all stats including defenses (Block, Dodge, Resistances) and Vit ❤️ are derived from the PC’s Atr. This means NPC Vit = PC’s 🫀 (NOT PC’s ❤️). And NPC 👣 = PC’s 🏃 (NOT PC’s 👣), etc.
* **Scaling Benefit:** As the player character advances and increases attribute ratings through AP, their companion entities scale automatically in lockstep without requiring bloated monster statblocks or manual sheet recalculations.
* **Unmodified Standard:** If an entity has no unusual modifications, simply default to Using PC Atr, and this completely defines the creature!

**Exceptions & Overrides:**

* Specific adaptations or specialized roles are expressed strictly as **bonuses or multipliers** of the PC’s Atr ratings, and occasionally as static numbers.
* **Vitality Overrides:** If Vitality differs from 🫀, express as a bonus (Vit 🫀+6) or a static pool (Vit 4).
* **Attack Overrides:** Melee attacks scale from Might (e.g. Atk 💪+2), Shot attacks scale from Mind (e.g. Atk 👁️/2), Hurled attacks scale from Motion (e.g. 🏃-2), and Magic attacks scale from Magic (✨+3).
* **Defense / Mobility Overrides:** Dodge adjustments modify Motion 🏃 (e.g. Def 🏃-2 or Def 🏃+2), and movement rates can be noted as custom speeds (👣 4 or 👣 10 Fly).

**Concrete Shorthand Examples:**

* **Standard Hunting Companion:** Summon Hunting Hound. NOTHING else needs to be specified as the default rule is ALWAYS in play: The companion inherits all of your Attribute ratings (Vit = 🫀, MR = 🏃). Simple!
* **Heavy Combat Guardian:** Summon Iron Golem (Vit 🫀+10, Atk 💪+2, Def 🏃-2, Dmg 💪+3, 👣 4).
* **Fragile Recon Drone:** Deploy Micro-Drone (Vit 4, Atk 👁️/2, Def 🏃+2, 👣 10 Fly).

## Part 5 · Weapons, Armor & Shields

The gear you fight with and the styles you fight in.

### 5.1 Weapons ⚔️

**Rolls:**

* **Unskilled Attacks —** Unknown weapons may be attempted as an Unskilled Attack (1d20 + d(Atr)) with normal damage and may make Unskilled Blocks.
* **Skilled Attacks —** Known weapons use a Skilled Attack (2H20 + d(Atr)) with normal damage and Skilled Blocks.

**Action:**

* **Attack & Block Costs —** Attacking is an Attack (A) action. Blocking is a Free (F) action.

**Multi Attacking:**

* **Single Weapon Example —** With 💪8 and a d4 Dagger, you can make 2 attacks (d4 + d4). With 12💪 d12, you can make 3 attacks (d4 + d4 + d4).
* **Dual Wielding Example —** With a d4 Dagger + d6 Shortsword (total d10), a 💪10 or 💪12 is required to make the multi-attack.
* **Multi-Attack Rules —** Roll a separate Attack and separate Dmg die for each strike. Strikes can target one foe or be split among adjacent foes.
* **⚠️ Uplifting Exception —** Multi-attack damage dice are NEVER uplifted to the Atr rating and are left at the requirement number (e.g. d4 and d6 in the above examples).

**Blocking Melee:**

* **Requirements —** Wielding a drawn MELEE weapon; monster attack is melee with incoming Dmg at or below the weapon’s Max Block rating.
* **Resolution —** Success = negates all damage. Failure = hit connects, roll Armor AR value to reduce damage.

#### 🎯 Weapon, Armor & Shield Requirements

A character can become skilled in any Weapon ⚔️ even if they do not yet meet the Atr requirement:

* **Learning Surcharge (+1 AP):** Learning a weapon without meeting its attribute requirement adds a +1 AP surcharge (a total of 2 AP In-Path, or 4 AP Out-of-Path with GM approval).
* **Current-Attribute Scaling:** The weapon's combat ratings (Attack die, Damage die, Block Cap) spec cleanly to the character's current Atr ratings.
* **Auto-Improvement (0 AP):** Anytime the character's relevant attribute advances, the weapon's attack and damage naturally scale to match the higher attribute for 0 AP.
* **AP Refund:** The extra AP (+1 for unmet requirements, and/or +2 for Out-of-Path acquisition) is fully refunded to the character's available AP pool as soon as the character meets the attribute requirement and/or acquires the parent Path.

### 5.2 Combat Styles

There are four primary combat styles in SupaFlex. By default, the core rules treat them similarly; distinctions and advantages mainly come from Powers 🔥.

* Single Weapon 🗡️ - Only usable during Single Weapon combat style (e.g. a single weapon with no shield)
* Dual Wield ⚔️ - Only usable during Dual Wield combat style (e.g. a weapon in each hand)
* Weapon & Shield 🛡️ - Only usable during Weapon & Shield combat style (e.g. a shield and one-handed weapon)
* Martial Arts🥋 - Only usable during Unarmed 🥊 combat style (e.g. Brawling)

| Combat Style | Off-Hand | Key Rules & Utility Handling |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **🗡️ Single Weapon** | FREE (1H) / TIED UP (2H) | **Maximum precision. 1H:** Off-hand is free for torches, flasks, or potions (utility tasks require a Partial [P] action). 2H: Utility tasks require a Move (M) action. |
| **⚔️ Dual Wield** | TIED UP | Hold two 1H weapons. Use higher Block rating of either. Utility tasks require a Move (M) action. |
| **🛡️ Weapon & Shield** | TIED UP | Hold 1H weapon + Shield. Shield Block allowed. Utility tasks require a Move (M) action. |
| **🥊 Unarmed / Natural** | FREE | Brawling, punches, kicks, grabs, thrown objects. Cannot Block. Damage is -1d below d(Atr) (min d4). |

#### 🗡️ Single Weapon

* Fighting with a single weapon ⚔️, one-handed or two-handed.
* Emphasizes precision and Powers 🔥. With a one-handed weapon ⚔️, your off-hand is free and utility tasks use a normal Partial (P) action; with a two-handed weapon ⚔️, utility tasks require a Move (M) action (temporarily shifting the weapon into one hand to use the other).

#### ⚔️ Dual Wield

* Fighting with two one-handed weapons ⚔️. Emphasizes speed and versatility.
* **Rules:**
* You may Block 🛡️ using the better weapon’s Block value.
* You may Atk ⚔️ with either weapon, but only one Atk ⚔️ per rnd unless a Power 🔥 allows more attacks or you use the default Multi-Attack rules.
* Two-handed weapons ⚔️ cannot be dual wielded.
* Utility tasks (opening doors, drinking potions, etc.) require a Move (M) action (sheath one weapon ⚔️, perform the action, retrieve the weapon ⚔️).

#### 🛡️ Weapon & Shield

* Fighting with a one-handed weapon ⚔️ in one hand and a shield 🛡️ in the other.
* Emphasizes balance between offense and defense.
* **Shields 🛡️ restrict the off-hand:** you cannot normally hold a torch or item in the shield hand and remain effective.
* Utility tasks (opening doors, drinking potions, etc.) require a Move (M) action (awkwardly holding the weapon ⚔️ with the shield 🛡️ hand while using the free hand).

#### 🥊 Unarmed or Natural Weapons

* Natural weapons 🥊 (Brawl and Throw Object) cannot Block 🛡️. They are Unskilled unless the weapon skill is taken. Dmg is rolled at –1d below the governing Atr✅ (minimum d4).
* **Brawl 🥊 —** Covers all natural attacks such as punch, kick, tackle, grab, throw, hold, etc. It also includes racial natural weapons such as claw, bite, or tail sweep.
* **Martial Arts🥋 —** An advanced form Brawl 🥊 provided through Powers 🔥.
* Improvised (Melee) - An improvised melee weapon (chair, board, candelabra, torch, etc.) that are NOT existing melee weapons fit into this category.
* **Throw Object —** Applies to all logical thrown projectiles such as a rock, mug, or other improvised items that are NOT existing hurled weapons.
* With GM 🔎 approval, non-hurled weapons ⚔️ (such as a short sword) may be thrown as improvised objects. In this case, all Throw Object rules apply.

#### ✋ Off-Hand Use & Utility Tasks

* **One-Handed or Unarmed (Free Off-Hand):** A free off-hand (when using a one-handed weapon ⚔️ or unarmed 🥊) allows carrying utility items such as a torch, lantern, Artifact 🔮, or flask, and performing utility tasks as a Partial (P) action.
* Two-Handed, Dual Wield, or Weapon & Shield 🛡️ (Occupied Hands): When using a two-handed weapon ⚔️, dual wielding, or using Weapon & Shield 🛡️, both hands are occupied. Performing a utility task (shifting a two-handed weapon into one hand, sheathing a weapon, or holding your weapon in your shield hand to open a door, drink a potion, etc.) requires a Move (M) action.

### 5.3 Armor 🧥 & Shields 🛡️

**Rolls:**

* **Unskilled Armor —** Unknown armor may be attempted as an Unskilled Dodge (1d20 + d🏃) and normal AR.
* **Skilled Armor —** Skilled armor uses a Skilled Dodge (2H20 + d🏃) and normal AR.

**Dodge vs Block:**

* **Attack Evasion —** All attacks may be Dodged; some attacks may be Blocked rather than Dodged (see weapon and shield rules).
* **Exclusive Defenses —** A character may not both Block and Dodge the same attack; it is an “Either-Or”.
* **AR 🧥 Protection —** If Dodge 🏃 or Block 🛡️ fail, the Armor's AR 🧥 is always rolled to reduce Dmg into Wnds.

**Action:**

* **Action Cost —** ALL dodge, block, and armor rolls are a Free (F) action.

#### 🧥 Armor & 🛡️ Shield Requirements

**Unmet Attribute Requirements:** Just like weapons (§5.1), you can learn any Armor 🧥 or Shield 🛡️ before meeting its Attribute ✅ requirement. It functions at your current Attribute ratings, carries a +1 AP learning surcharge (+2 AP more if Out-of-Path with GM approval), scales automatically for 0 AP as your Attribute grows, and refunds the surcharge AP as soon as you meet the requirement or unlock its Path.

#### 🛡️ Shields

**Rolls:**

* **Unskilled Shield Block —** Unknown shields may be attempted as an Unskilled Block (1d20 + d💪).
* **Skilled Shield Block:** Known shields use a Skilled Block (2H20 + d💪).

**Action:**

* **Action Cost —** Blocking is a Free (F) action.

MR 👣

* MR 👣 impact applies only when the shield 🛡️ is wielded (drawn and readied).
* Large shields 🛡️ provide greater Block Cap 🛡️ protection but impose greater limits on MR 👣.
* Bracers, bucklers, and other very small shields are considered part of the AR 🧥 and are not listed in Shields 🛡️.

**Blocking:**

* **Requirements:** Wielding a drawn shield; incoming Dmg is at or below the shield’s Max Block rating; applies to any attack that can be blocked (all melee, hurled, and shot weapons, plus any GM-approved ability attacks).
* The key advantage of shields 🛡️ is that their Block Cap 🛡️ is larger than melee weapon blocks and they can block ANY melee, hurled, shot attack and any other ability the GM determines is “blockable” (e.g. certain Powers 🔥). The tradeoff is reduced MR 👣 and tying up your off-hand.
* **Resolution —** Success = negates all damage. Failure = hit connects, roll Armor AR 🧥 value to reduce damage.

**Shield-Hand Restrictions:**

* **Off-Hand Limits —** Holding a shield completely ties up your off-hand. You cannot hold a torch, lantern, flask, or second weapon in your shield hand.
* **Utility Action Cost —** Performing utility tasks during combat (quaffing a potion, retrieving an item, opening a heavy door) requires a Move (M) action to awkwardly manage items while holding your weapon and shield.

### 5.4 Owning What You're Skilled In

* Weapons ⚔️, Armor 🧥, and Shields 🛡️ are all categories of **Gear ⚙️ AND all of them are also a skill 🎓**. Thus a character can learn Sword Skill 🎓 AND needs to own a Sword or the skill is not currently useful. On the other hand, ANY character can pick up a sword and use it as an unskilled weapon, in which case the character has the Gear ⚙️ “sword” but is NOT skilled at swords.
* **Default Gear Possession Rule:** Under SupaFlex’s default KISS rules, all common and obvious tools — as well as any Weapon ⚔️, Armor 🧥, or Shield 🛡️ selected and listed on the character sheet (regardless of whether Skilled or Unskilled) — are assumed to be physically owned as Gear ⚙️ without needing to specifically purchase them in the Gear Manager (unless the GM determines otherwise based on campaign tone and narrative context).

## Part 6 · Powers, Exotics & Treasure

How to read a power, how often you can use it, and where Powers 🔥 come from (Paths 🧭 and Exotics 🧿).

### 6.1 How to Read a Power 🔥

#### Power Grammar

To ensure absolute clarity without rulebook arguments or bloat, every Character and Exotic Power 🔥 follows a standardized, single-sentence Grammar:

**Name | Action | Usage | Effect**

* **Discordant Chord** | A **|** 1-Rnd | Atk ✨^all Short foes for d4 Dmg.
* **Cyclone Kick** | A **|** 1-⚡| Atk 🏃 Dmg d🏃; Hit = 💪^💪 = pushes target d4 sq.
* **Blade Parry** | F **|** 2-Enc | On hit in Melee = Auto-succeed a weapon Block 🛡️ (up to Block Cap).
* **Bardic Bluff** | P **|** 1-Enc | Mind 👁️^👁️ with Advantage = deceive or distract.
* **Frost Pebble (Artifact)** | A **|** 1-Enc | Rng Short; Atk 🏃^Def Cold Dmg d✨+4.

All abilities have an Action 🔷, Usage 🔄, Duration ⏳, and Range 🎯. Often these are self-evident and do not need verbose explanation. The only abilities that will explicitly list Action and Usage are Powers 🔥, but ALL abilities have them. If a Power’s Dur ⏳ and Rng 🎯 are not obvious they will be listed in the Effects.

**Range 🎯:** see §4.8 for the 9 Range Bands and Area of Effect rules.

#### ⏳ Duration (Dur)

* If a duration is listed, it will appear directly within the ability’s “Effect”. Often the Dur is obvious and not listed. The default for non-obvious durations is **1 Rnd** (ends on the character/monster's next round’s Nish 🚩).
* **Design Goal:** Minimal tracking. Durations are kept strictly to obvious (Instant, 1 Rnd, Encounter, or a single "next..." action). Multi-round numeric tracking (e.g. "for 3 rounds") is strictly prohibited.
* The full collection of officially permitted Durations in SupaFlex is:
  * **Instant:** Resolves immediately upon execution with no lingering state (typical for attacks, damage, or sudden shifts; obvious and often omitted).
  * **This Rnd:** Persists through the remainder of the current combat round, concluding when the GM calls for new Nish rolls.
  * **1 Rnd (The Universal Default):** Effect begins immediately and concludes upon the character/monster's next round’s Nish 🚩.
  * **Encounter:** Persists throughout the active encounter, concluding when the GM declares the encounter over.
  * **Next …:** This is a Trigger duration that begins on the specified upcoming roll or event, expiring immediately after that event resolves (e.g. next Def, next Atk, next Save, next Skill check) and does NOT extend past the current encounter, without GM approval.

### 6.2 Usage 🔄

**How often a Power 🔥 can be used:**

| Usage Option | Refresh & Mechanical Execution | Pacing & Table Utility |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1-⚡** | **1 Bolt:** Consumes 1 Bolt (5 Sparks, ⚡). | High-impact tactical burst, overdrive munitions, combat elixirs, combos, or battlefield supremacy. |
| **1-🍀** | **1 Luck:** Consumes 1 Luck 🍀 from player's pool. | Clutch cinematic interrupts, heroic saves, and auto-defenses. |
| **1-Enc** | **1 per Encounter:** Refreshes when combat/roleplaying encounter resolves. | Major tactical abilities, special stances, and defensive shields. |
| **2-Enc** | **2 per Encounter:** Refreshes when combat/roleplaying encounter resolves. | Flexible encounter abilities and tactical weapon maneuvers. |
| **3-Enc** | **3 per Encounter:** Refreshes when combat/roleplaying encounter resolves. | High-frequency encounter utilities and reliable tactical powers. (HARD CAP: SupaFlex will NEVER support more than 3 uses per encounter). |
| **1-Rnd** | **1 per Round:** Refreshes every round on Initiative (Nish 🚩). | Core base abilities, baseline spells, stance toggles, and primary combat actions. |

#### ⚡ Bolt Rules & Spark Generation

* **5 Sparks = 1 Bolt ⚡:** It ALWAYS takes 5 Sparks to build 1 Bolt ⚡. All Bolt abilities indicate this in the Usage column as 1-⚡ (representing 1 Bolt). There are no 2-⚡ or 3-⚡ costs.
* **Spark Generation Events:** Building a Bolt occurs via Sparks earned in gameplay:
* **Tremendous or Critical Rolls:** Gain +1 Spark for every natural 20 or natural 1 on the base d20 die of any ability roll.
* **Exploding Die Events:** Gain +1 Spark per exploding die roll (if a die explodes 4 times in a row, gain 4 Sparks).
* **Full Bolt State (+1 to All Rolls):** A character holding 5 Sparks has "A Full Bolt ⚡". While holding a full Bolt, they gain a flat +1 bonus to ALL Attribute derived rolls, including Defenses, and Damage rolls until they spend their Bolt ⚡ or the encounter ends.
* **Encounter Transition & Zeroing Out:**
  * If an encounter ends with the character at full Bolt (5 Sparks), then the Bolt and all Sparks are lost (reset to 0).
  * Otherwise, the number of current Sparks persist into the next encounter. (Characters are continuously transitioning between encounter types, whether combat or roleplaying).

**ℹ️ Note:**

* High-tier powers use Bolt (1-⚡) or 1-🍀.
* Once per encounter, a character can spend a Luck 🍀 to activate any known Power 🔥 (including Exotic 🧿 Powers 🔥), regardless of its Usage (1-🍀, 1-⚡, 1-Enc, 2-Enc, 3-Enc, or 1-Rnd), unless the GM chooses to approve more.

### 6.3 Exotics 🧿, Mods 🔌 & Artifacts 🔮

**Learning & AP Costs:**

* **Exotic 🧿 Mods 🔌 —** Inherent Mods are free, others mods cost g/s. Exotic
* **Exotic 🧿 Powers 🔥 —** Exotic Powers Cost 1 AP to learn.

**Exotics 🧿:**

* **An Exotic 🧿 piece of gear ⚙️ MUST have a Mod, and all Exotic Powers must have a Mod. It is not possible for an Exotic power to exist without a Mod to belong to. There is a mod class “Inherent” that is the free (no g/s cost) and fundamental mod of Exotic 🧿 Gear ⚙️.**

**Mods 🔌:**

* **Subordinate & Modular:** Represents a module, aftermarket installation, or physical hardware upgrade to the parent Gear ⚙️. (Note: the parent Gear ⚙️ is called the Chassis).
* **Mod cost:** Mods carry a financial purchase cost (g/s), except for the factory-installed “Inherent” mod.

**Exotic 🧿 Powers 🔥:**

* *Exotic***🧿** *Powers 🔥 use the same rules as ability Powers 🔥:* (Action, Usage, Effect, etc.). and cost **1 AP** to learn, but can ONLY be used if the parent Exotic Gear (the Chassis) is owned and being used.

#### Exotics 🧿 Overview

| 🧿 Exotic Gear Chassis | Mod Type | Mod Acquisition & Origin | Powers (1 AP) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Weapons ⚔️, Armor 🧥, Shields 🛡️, Supplies 🎒** | 📦 INHERENT MOD | Built directly into the factory gear chassis (comes stock with the exotic item). | 🔥 Power (1 AP) Learned as an active ability via character AP. |
| **Weapons ⚔️, Armor 🧥, Shields 🛡️, Supplies 🎒** | 🔌 INSTALLED MOD | Purchased aftermarket with gold/silver (g/s) and socketed into available mod capacity. | 🔥 Power (1 AP) Learned as an active ability via character AP. |

#### The Universal Learning Model & Ownership Lineage

* **Physical Ownership Gate:** Characters cannot browse Artifacts 🔮 but must find them as loot. To access an Exotic 🧿 the character must physically own the host gear — meaning the Exotic Gear is either owned in the character's Gear ⚙️ Manager or selected and listed on the character sheet in the Weapons ⚔️, Armor 🧥, or Shields 🛡️ cards (regardless of whether Skilled or Unskilled).
* **1 AP Learning:** Every Exotic 🧿 Power 🔥 costs exactly 1 AP to learn.
* **Installed Mod:** Exotic 🧿 Powers 🔥 are tied to a specific Mod 🔌 requiring that mod to be installed on the host gear before the power can be learned.
* **Unlearn / AP Refund:** Players may unlearn a Power or remove a Mod (and thus all of its Powers) at any time, immediately refunding all related AP.
* Deleting/Dropping an exotic immediately refunds 100% of invested AP.

### 6.4 Chaos Gauntlet & Gems 💎

The **Chaos Gauntlet** is an astral, ethereal, arcane harness capable of containing and wielding volatile Chaos Gems. Normally the gauntlet is invisible and intangible, only appearing when the wielder wills it so. It features **six Chaos Gem slots**:

* 👑 **Wrist "Mega Slot" (1 Slot):** The primary focal slot of the gauntlet.
* **🖐️ Finger Slots (5 Slots):** Thumb, Index, Middle, Ring, and Pinky slots.
* **Gem Custody:** Chaos Gems are volatile, attuned crystallizations that cannot be carried loosely in pockets, backpacks, or pouches. They may only exist when socketed directly into a Chaos Gauntlet.
* **Permanent Destruction on Removal:** Sockets form an irrevocable metaphysical fusion upon insertion. Removing a gem from a Chaos Gauntlet immediately shatters and destroys it.

**Usage & Destruction at Zero:**

* **Chaos Gem Finite Durability:** Socketed Chaos Gauntlet gems strictly retain 3 finite uses and will auto-shatter upon the 3rd use.
* **Wrist Mega Slot** allows spending a Bolt (1-⚡) to activate a gem without consuming a charge.

**Action Economy & Rate of Use:**

* Activating a Chaos Gem is a **Free Action (F)**.

### 6.5 Treasure, Essence & Disenchanting 🧪

If the encounter does not list specific treasure, a loot roll is typically made after any typical encounter or after any encounter that would warrant treasure. If the encounter was minor, skip the roll; if major, allow multiple rolls. GMs will find a treasure balance that works for their campaign.

#### 🧪 Essence & Disenchanting Engine

Unwanted loot drops may be disenchanted directly into personal **Essence Core** progress. The Essence Flask utilizes a **Graduated Overcharge** architecture:

* **Basic Infusion (50% Essence):** Reaching 50% Essence allows a player to synthesize a single Artifact roll. The player may claim the Artifact to their sheet (consuming all Essence) or deconstruct it (cutting current Essence in half to 25%).
* **Overcharged Master Synthesis (100% Essence):** Saving the flask until it is 100% full unlocks the Master 3-Card Smart Draft (offering two distinct Artifact options plus one Art & Gem Treasure Cache). The player selects one reward to claim to their sheet (consuming 100% Essence) or may deconstruct the draft (cutting current Essence in half to 50%).

##### Disenchant Recycling Ratios

**Disenchanting an item returns Essence based on item category:**

| Item / Drop Type | Disenchant Yield |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Standard Gear / Valuables** | +8% |

## Part 7 · Advancement ⭐

**How your hero grows:** levels, Adventure Points, and what to spend them on.

### 7.1 Levels & Adventure Points 🧩

**⭐ Leveling & 🧩 AP**

* Gain 2 AP per Level
* Free Vit Roll
* AP Free Max Vit Roll (Roll & keep higher) per level.

#### Advancement Philosophy

* **Incremental, not exponential →** Growth comes from new abilities, not huge Ability spikes or giant numerical increases.
* **Ability > Dice →** The real fun is in unique Powers 🔥, gear, and choices—not just bigger dice.
* **Cinematic growth →** Luck, quirks, and Powers 🔥 drive memorable play moments.
* **Customization first →** Players shape advancement to match their character’s story, not a rigid track.

### 7.2 Max Vit Roll

At each Level ⭐, roll for a new maximum Vit ❤️. This is an AP 🧩 free roll:

* Levels 1–3: 10+1d🫀
* Levels 4–8: 10+2d🫀
* Levels 9–15: 10+3d🫀
* Levels 16–24: 10+4d🫀
* **Levels 25+:** 10+5d🫀 (HARD CAP — Prevents late-game Vit ❤️ bloat)
* This is like “Lucking 🍀” Max Vit ❤️ (roll a new number and keep the better of the old Vit ❤️ or the new Vit ❤️). If your Moxie 🫀 has increased, you use the new Moxie 🫀 value in the formula.

### 7.4 No AP Is Ever Lost

* When your character unlocks a new Path (4 AP 🧩), check every ability you already own (Powers, Skills, Weapon, Armor, and Shield Proficiencies, and Traits). Any ability tagged with the new Path that you bought at a higher Out-of-Path price (e.g. 3 AP 🧩 or 4 AP 🧩) is re-priced to its In-Path cost (1 AP or 2 AP), and the difference is immediately returned to your available AP.
* Unlearning (removing) an ability always refunds its full AP cost.
* If an AP 🧩 purchased Skill becomes part of a learned Skill Set, the purchase of the standalone Skill is similarly refunded.
* If a weapon ⚔️, armor 🧥, shield 🛡️ was learned for an extra +1AP 🧩 due to the character not meeting Atr requirements, and that character later meets those requirements, the extra 1 AP 🧩 is similarly refunded.
* **Bottom line rule:** No AP 🧩 is ever lost or wasted.

## Part 8 · For the Game Master 🔎

Tools and guidance for running the game. Players may skip this Part.

### 8.1 GM Principles

* **Say Yes:** If the idea is fun, let it ride or lower Dif.
* **Failures Push Forward:** Failures cause complications, never dead ends.
* **Spotlight Cool:** Highlight Strengths, Flairs, and Creative Powers 🔥.
* **Keep Monsters Simple:** Use flat stats, improvise Powers 🔥, focus on PCs’ rolls.

### 8.2 GM Intensity Die

The GM 🔎 should keep a single d20 for quick, one-off results. This die never uses bonuses, multiple dice, or add-ons. It provides a snap judgment when randomness or intensity is needed.

**Uses:**

**Critical and Tremendous Intensity:**

* **High rolls (toward 20):** Means greater intensity — more impactful results (good for PCs in Tremendous cases, bad in Criticals).
* **Low rolls (toward 1):** Means minimal intensity, possibly even canceling the effect.

**Answering quick questions:**

* Do I see any throwable rocks nearby?
* Does the pickpocketed target have coins?
* Does my rope hold 500 lbs?

**Examples resolving NPC vs NPC actions without full rolls:**

* **Atk Intensity (18) →** hits.
* **Dmg Intensity (10) →** causes 3 Wnd 🩸s❤️.
* **Def Intensity (8) →** hit; AR Intensity (19) → blocks all Dmg.
* **AR Intensity (20) →** blocks all Dmg and disarms opponent.
* **NOTE:** An experienced GM can do all of the above in just 1 or 2 rolls rather than the 5 rolls indicated.

### 8.3 Setting Difficulty

**When setting or modifying Dif, the GM should weigh:**

* The skill’s name and scope.
* The character’s background.
* The creativity of the player’s narrative approach.
* The role of the skill in the adventure/campaign.
* Any unusual effects that help or hinder.
* Overall fairness and balance in play.
* Dif should reflect both the task’s challenge and the quality of the player’s solution.
* Yes, the GM setting a difficulty is already the default rule and well explained. However, be VERY careful about slipping into numeric based logic for coming up with difficulties as this will often trap you into a much more complex (and often unbalanced) and nearly always non-cinematic/heroic game. For example, if a PC has Acrobatics skill, do not set the difficulty of vaulting over a 6ft opponent at X*2*ft and leaping every 5ft of distance as Y*sq, etc. This may seem like a good idea at first, but it rarely ends up being a good rule. Rather, set a difficulty for the vault or leap or acrobatic attempt based upon the situation and the character’s concept. This is VERY open ended, subjective, and will add much more flavor to your game as players learn that they can do things like, “I’m going to try to run down the hall, parcour run 5ft up that wall leap over that orc with a twisting vault and then land in front of the next Orc. The GM then assigns a single difficulty and the player makes a single roll. Much more cinematic, creative, open and does not lock you into looking up detailed tables or rules.
* Another reason for this is different GMs or settings, or games. If you are playing a more heroic (super hero) game, then climbing a smooth steel wall may be a low Dif. However, if you are playing a hyper realistic WWII based game, that same wall may be impossible or nearly so. Tabling up such detail is rarely a good idea.
* Digging deeper on this topic, climbing difficulties or rules such as a player must check every 10 or 50 or 100ft and that a rough vs smooth vs stone vs glass wall all have some defined Dif or modifier, gets very messy very fast. Rather, assess the situation (the desired climb, the character, the setting) and assign a Dif. You and your players will usually prefer this approach over artificial rigidity.

### 8.4 GM Tricks

This section covers a number of great GM tricks and examples to help your game run even more smoothly. All tricks/rules herein are optional and may be incorporated into your game sessions at the GM’s discretion. Some of these tricks are more advanced rules that can be incorporated once the GM and their group has fully grasped the base rules.

#### Helper - Bleeder

Assign one player as the “Bleeder.” The Bleeder is a GM helper whose job it is to track all monster wounds for the GM. For example, if you use a write on board or mat, give them a red pen and if Orc A takes 5 Wnds 🩸 it is the Bleeder’s responsibility to place a 5 next to Orc A. If that orc later takes another 3 Wnds 🩸, the Bleeder will change the 5 to 8. Also as you, the GM, move the monster around the board, it is the Bleeder’s responsibility to erase the old Wnd 🩸 number and place it adjacent to the monster’s new location. Finally, you can assign the Bleeder (if they are an experienced gamer) or some other helper to track monster death. This helper will let everyone know when a monster should die when its Wnds 🩸 reach or exceed its Vit ❤️. Once you train your Bleeder up, you are largely freed up, as GM, from this common set of tasks.

#### Random Direction

Often you will need to determine a random direction such as when someone is disarmed or if a hurled item misses. A great way to do this is to have the involved player roll a d12 to represent the hours of a clock (ALWAYS from the GM’s point of view) thus a 12 is away from the GM, 3 is to the right, 6 toward the GM, 9 to the left. And the other numbers are some combination therein. If you also need a random distance, just have the player roll an appropriate die (d6, d8, 2H6, etc.) or you can roll your GM’ s Intensity die.

#### Falling, Climbing, Jumping Down

* Simple version - when a character falls, the GM assigns an amount of Dmg (e.g. 10) and the player rolls AR 🧥 vs that Dmg. There is no Def as you cannot block or dodge the ground - it will Hit you!
* I roughly base the Dmg on feet fallen and landing surface (bolder field vs spikes vs sand vs water, etc.). I also reduce the Dmg if the jump was intentional or if the character hung down from a ledge and then dropped the rest of the distance, etc. Finally, realize that setting falling Dmg at 20 is much worse than setting it at 10 as few AR 🧥 rolls can absorb Dmg 20, but larger armor can absorb most or all of Dmg 10 on a good roll. So Dmg is NOT linear from a character’s point of view.

#### Avoid Auto-Hazards

Yes, there are a number of auto-hit, or “PC takes Dmg with no AR 🧥” etc., but these are pre-build, typed rules, and your players cannot blame you the GM for “conspiring against them” or treating them unfair. That said, in general avoid an auto-hazard, auto-negative effect, or auto-affliction as this justifiably will frustrate your players as they then have no “control” over these bad effects occurring. The default rule is, a PC should always get at least a single roll to avoid a bad effect (typically a Block, Dodge, or Save). So rather than have a player walking down a sewer tunnel suddenly fall into a 15ft pit, give them an Awareness check. Or if a trapped chest releases poisonous gas, give them a Poison Save, etc. Falling from a climb is already covered as the character had to have failed their climbing roll. Even a character pushed off a cliff had a chance as they failed their Dodge or Block (or Save) attempt versus that which pushed them.

#### Character Introduction

At the start of each game session, it is a great idea to go around the table and have each player introduce (or re-introduce) their character to the group. They should point out their miniature or token (if used) and then describe what their character looks like (race, height, weight, hair, eyes, features) and explain demeanor/behavior. And at a minimum explain what their character’s main role (class) will be, or if they characters have a history together, they explain their characters main abilities and common strategies.

### 8.5 Nish Methods

There are several methods the GM can choose between when running Nish (PC and Monster Nishes). The tradeoffs are between detail and individual flow and speed of each round. There is not a best approach. Rather each approach has different pros/cons and the GM needs to select the best approach for their current gaming group and campaign.

#### Example

For each of the methods below, we’ll use the example of Nish rolls as follows:

* PCs
  * Warrior Nish roll of 16
  * Thief Nish roll of 8
  * Mage Nish roll of 2
* Monsters
  * Orc Guards Nish of 14
  * Old Kobold Nish of 5

#### Method 1 - Default - Individual Nishes

* Individual Nishes means that the GM calls on the highest Nish to lowest Nish in order, one by one, including all PCs and all monster types. So using the above example, the Nish sequence would be:
* Warrior at 16
* Orc Guards at 14
* Thief at 8
* Old Kobold at 5
* Mage at 2
* **Pros:** This is the most structured, everyone gets their turn, each monster type goes on their own Nish which may feel more natural.
* **Cons:** This will be the slowest method, particularly for complex characters or new players. Also the hardest for the GM to track.

#### Method 2 - Group Monsters

* When grouping monsters, all monsters go on the best monster’s Nish. So using the above example, the Nish sequence would be:
* Warrior at 16
* All monsters at 14
* Thief at 8
* Mage at 2
* **Pros:** GM gets to decide all monster actions at once so it is much easier for the GM to manage. Also creates a bit of strategy for players to determine which monsters are the high Nish group and try to take them out to reduce the monster group Nish.
* **Cons:** Give up a little bit of realism that a slow monster type goes later in the round and a fast monster type goes earlier.

#### Method 3 - Groups

* With the Group method, all PCs who beat the highest Nish monsters all go at once, then all monsters, then all PCs who have Nishes below the best monster Nish. When the PC groups go, each group (beat monsters or were slower than monsters) all roll their abilities simultaneously, write them down, and when they are “Ready to Tell Their Story” the GM calls on them and they say something like, “I swung my sword at the Orc, hit, and slashed the orc for 7 Dmg which is 6 Wnds” Then the GM moves on to the next PC story. So using the above example, the Nish sequence would be:
* All PCs with Nishes above 14 go simultaneously (Warrior at 16)
* All monsters at 14
* All PCs with Nishes below 14 go simultaneously (Thief and Mage)
* **Pros:** The fastest method. Chaos of players rolling simultaneously (perhaps against the same target) can simulate the chaos of real battle.
* **Cons:** Requires experienced players who know what to roll and when. A monster may get slain by PC A making a simultaneous attack by PC B seem like a waste. But a good GM can explain that both PC A and B hit the monster simultaneously.

### 8.6 Skill Negotiation Examples

#### 🧗 Example 1 – Climbing a Sheer Cliff

* **Basic Use:**
* **Player:** “I want to climb that 100ft sheer cliff.”
* **GM 🔎:** “OK, Dif 24.”
* **Negotiated Use:**
* **Player:** “If I use my climbing 🎓 to plan a route first, can I lower the Dif?”
* **GM 🔎:** “Yes — make your roll… the Dif is now 18 with planning.”
* **Alternate Approach:**
* **Player:** “I’ll climb in two stages, using rope, iron spikes, and a mallet. I’ll move at half MR 👣 for safety.”
* **GM 🔎:** “First roll to the ledge (Dif 14). If you fail, you’ll get a second roll to catch yourself on the rope instead of falling. Then we’ll proceed with your final ascent.”

#### 🕳️ Example 2 – Falling into a Pit

* **Basic Use:**
* **GM 🔎:** “You fall into a pit trap, having failed your Save 🏃. Make your AR 🧥 check vs Dmg10.”
* **Player:** “I have falling 🎓. I rolled X.”
* **GM 🔎:** “OK, damage is reduced to 8.”
* **Narrative Detail:**
* **Player:** “On the way down I pick my landing, prepare my body to absorb impact, and grab anything to slow the fall. I’m also a Highland Monk used to mountaineering. I rolled the same X.”
* **GM 🔎:** “Good description — damage is reduced to 4. Make your AR 🧥 check.”

#### ⚕️ Example 3 – Healing Fredo

* **Basic Use:**
* **Player:** “I use healing 🎓 (15) on Fredo at –1 Vit ❤️.”
* **GM 🔎:** “You stop bleeding and stabilize him, but he remains critical.”
* **Narrative Detail:**
* **Player:** “Fredo took a cleaver blow to the arm. I splint, bandage, and stabilize, hoping to heal +1 Vit ❤️. I trained for decades in the Temple ward.”
* **GM 🔎:** “Dif 10 to both stop bleeding and restore +1 Vit ❤️.”

#### 🥷 Example 4 – Sneaking Past Orc Guards

* **Basic Use:**
* **Player:** “Stealth 🎓 12 to slip past the two Orc guards.” GM 🔎: “Their Mind 👁️ is 13. You fail.”
* **Narrative Detail:**
* **Player:** “I study the hallway and guards, planning my best route. I toss a pebble to the far side to distract them.”
* **GM 🔎:** “OK — roll Stealth 🎓 vs Dif 9.”

#### ⚔️ Example 5 – Battlefield Tactics

* **Basic Use:**
* **Player:** “Battlefield Tactics 🎓 15. I want +1 Atk ⚔️ for the party this whole encounter.”
* **GM 🔎:** “That’s too broad. Try a smaller effect.”
* **Narrative Detail:**
* **Player:** “I analyze the Troll’s armor and movements to help Fredo’s dagger strike. I want +2 Dmg on his next attack vs that Troll.”
* **GM 🔎:** “Open roll vs Dif 13.”
* **Player:** “Rolled 19.”
* **GM 🔎:** “That’s excellent. Fredo gets +3 Dmg on his next dagger hit — but remember, skills 🎓 support narrative moments. They don’t replace Powers 🔥.”

#### 👑 Example 6 – Persuasion in the Great Hall

* **Basic Use (One Big Roll):**
* **Player:** “I stride into the great hall, raise my voice, and make my plea to everyone at once.”
* **GM 🔎:** “That’s ambitious. One Persuasion 👁️ roll vs Dif 20 to sway the entire hall.”
* **Result →** Success means broad support; failure means near-universal rejection.
* **Narrative Detail (Split & Conquer):**
* **Player:** “Instead of talking to the entire hall, I mingle among smaller groups. I quietly approach three key nobles — some listen, some dismiss me. Then I use those connections to build momentum before addressing everyone.”
* **GM 🔎:** “OK, make individual Persuasion 👁️ rolls with each noble. -- Some succeed, some fail, but one is an influential court member.”
* **Player:** “Now I address the full hall, with that noble backing me.”
* **GM 🔎:** “Because of the groundwork, your final roll is with Advantage (3H20) and the Dif is reduced to 15 instead of 20.”
* **Result →** Success has greater weight due to noble support; failure may still leave cracks of doubt but not total rejection.

## Appendix A · Optional Rules

Appendix A collects optional rules your GM may include to add extra tactical depth, weapon variety, or combat momentum.

### Damage Shift (Dmg Shift)

Damage Shift is an optional weapon combat rule that allows large, heavy weapons to deliver devastating impact on accurate strikes, while granting light, agile weapons desperate finesse to turn near-misses into grazing hits.

#### 1. Heavy Weapon Shift (Atk ➔ Dmg)

Weapons with damage dice **d6 through d12** can shift excess Attack roll margin over target Defense into flat bonus Damage (+ Dmg).

* **Max Shift Cap:** The maximum amount of Attack you can shift into Damage depends on the weapon's base damage die:
  * **d4:** 0 (cannot shift Atk to Dmg; see Reverse Shift below)
  * **d6:** Max +1
  * **d8:** Max +2
  * **d10:** Max +3
  * **d12:** Max +4 (Max Shift = (Die Size - 4) / 2)
* **Resolution:** When an attack hits over target Defense, the player may convert excess attack margin up to the weapon's Max Shift into + Dmg. For example, with a d8 weapon (Max Shift 2): beating Defense by 1 adds +1 Dmg; beating Defense by 2 or more adds +2 Dmg.
* **Stacking:** Dmg Shift stacks with everything.

#### 2. Light Weapon Reverse Shift (Dmg ➔ Atk — d4 Weapons ONLY)

Weapons with base damage **d4** (and ONLY d4 weapons, such as daggers, knuckles, and darts) can shift in the opposite direction.

* **Desperate Finesse:** If a player misses an Attack roll by 4 or less, they can call a Dmg Shift to consider the attack a hit, but they must subtract that equivalent deficit from their rolled Damage.
* **Example:** If an Attack roll is 17 vs. Defense 20 (missing by 3), the player calls Dmg Shift: the strike hits, but applies -3 to the Damage roll.
* **Damage & Wounds Threshold:** The maximum d4 shift is 4. If shifting results in 0 or negative damage, then Dmg is 0 and nothing happens (no wounds occur).

| Weapon Base Dmg Die | Max Shift Cap | Shift Direction | Core Rule Summary |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **d4** | 4 | Dmg ➔ Atk ONLY | **Reverse Finesse:** Convert a miss by up to 4 into a hit, subtracting the difference from Dmg (minimum 0 Dmg). |
| **d6** | +1 | Atk ➔ Dmg | Beat Defense by 1 or more ➔ add +1 Dmg. |
| **d8** | +2 | Atk ➔ Dmg | Beat Defense by 1 ➔ +1 Dmg; by 2 or more ➔ +2 Dmg. |
| **d10** | +3 | Atk ➔ Dmg | Beat Defense by 1, 2, or 3+ ➔ add up to +3 Dmg. |
| **d12** | +4 | Atk ➔ Dmg | Beat Defense by 1, 2, 3, or 4+ ➔ add up to +4 Dmg. |

### 🚩 +1 Nish Reward

When running combat encounters, the GM may optionally reward swift reflexes:

* **Rule:** If a PC rolls higher than ALL monsters in the encounter on their Initiative (Nish 🚩) check, that PC gains a +1 bonus on all rolls until the monsters take their first turn.
* **Stacking:** Stacks with everything.

### 🏹 Ranged Attack into Melee

* A great optional rule (for the experienced GM) is to place risk (danger) anytime a ranged weapon (hurled or shot) is used at a target engaged in melee. In the real world, melee is a highly agitated state of micro moves, dodges, etc. and it is very hard to target your intended victim and easy to hit your ally. So, the rule is, if that attacker misses the desired enemy target (their Atk roll fails), they have a 50/50 chance of hitting their melee involved ally. Simply have them roll a d6 and 1-3 = Atk auto-hits ally (Roll normal Dmg and Ally rolls normal AR 🧥).

### 🎯 Extended Range (Ext Rng)

* **Extended Range (Ext Rng):** With GM approval, a character may attack or use a weapon, ability, or item one range band beyond its normal listed Range Band 🎯 (e.g. Short ➔ Medium, or Medium ➔ Long) at Disadvantage.

## Appendix B · Writing Custom Effects

This appendix provides player-facing and GM guidelines for authoring custom ability **Effects.**

* **Succinct Rules-Dense Shorthand:** Effect descriptions are NOT conversational English prose. They are ultra-condensed, rules-dense summaries of actionable mechanics. Never use filler phrasing such as "This device allows the user to..." or "When activated, the player may...".
* **Strict Separation of Notes vs. Effects:**
  * **Notes:** Reserved exclusively for tactile physical descriptions, narrative worldbuilding, lore flavor, and non-combat utility. Base notes must NEVER contain combat math, die codes, Caret checks, or status condition triggers.
  * **Effects:** Reserved exclusively for mechanical rules, Dur, Rng, dice, action economy, ranges, Caret checks, afflictions, and condition applications.
* **No Redundant Action or Usage Badges:** Action types (AM, A, M, P, F) and Usages (1-⚡, 1-🍀, 1-Enc, 2-Enc, 3-Enc, 1-Rnd) are tracked in dedicated application columns and UI badges. Never repeat action costs or usage limits in the effect text itself.
* **Zero Resource Tracking:** Never track bullet counts, ammo magazines, battery percentages, or fuel tallies. SupaFlex operates on clean, cinematic resource tracking.

### 🎨 The Canon Icons

Custom element design enforces a whitelist of universal icons used in Effects typically limited to: ✨💪👁️🏃🫀🚩👣🍀❤️

### 🔗 Causal Consequence

* When an attack or trigger directly produces an automatic mechanical consequence, link the sequence with an equals sign (=) or the word “or”:
* Atk ✨^Def or target levitated and dropped for fall Dmg.
* Wnds = target Stunned.
* Do not use ambiguous semicolons (;) to separate direct cause-and-effect sequences. Use semicolons only to separate independent, distinct effects.

## Appendix C · Glossary & Symbols

This appendix serves as the definitive reference glossary and symbolic lexicon for SupaFlex. It codifies all core mechanics, action categories, status conditions, and game iconography to maintain an authoritative, unambiguous design baseline.

### C.1 Glossary (A–Z)

Every SupaFlex term and abbreviation in one A–Z list. Each heading shows the § where the full rule lives.

#### Ability 🧠 (§2.3)

* **Abilities 🧠 —** Any intangible, learned capability unlocked with Adventure Points (AP 🧩) and organized into Paths 🧭. Categorized strictly into three functional groups: Traits 🧬, Powers 🔥, and Skills 🎓.

#### Ability Roll (§1.2)

* **Formula:** Roll #d20 + d(Atr) + Bonus versus a Difficulty (Dif) to determine success or failure. The number of d20 is governed by Skilled (2H20) vs. Unskilled (1d20) and Advantage vs. Disadvantage.

#### Action Economy 🔷 (§4.3)

* **Round Action Pool (AM, A, M, P, F):** The structured 4-channel budget governing action choices each round:
  * **Attack & Move (AM):** Consumes both the Attack and Move action (used for running/sprinting or heavy full-round maneuvers).
  * **Attack (A):** Standard offensive strike, spell cast, or active power action.
  * **Move (M):** Tactical movement up to your full Movement Rate (MR 👣) or mobility power, OR performing a utility task while using a two-handed weapon, dual wielding, or Weapon & Shield.
  * **Partial (P):** Preparatory utility with a free hand (drawing a weapon, donning a shield, quaffing a potion, opening a door, skill check).
  * **Free (F):** Instantaneous actions/reactions (Block, Dodge, AR rolls, verbal shouts, dropping items). Unlimited per round at GM discretion.

#### Adventure Points (AP 🧩)

* **AP 🧩 (Adventure Points):** The universal progression currency of SupaFlex. Two AP are gained per level and spent to unlock Paths 🧭, learn new abilities 🧠, or improve stats.

#### Advantage & Disadvantage (§1.3)

* **Adv / Dis:** The core dice variance mechanic on Ability Rolls:
  * **Advantage (Adv):** Roll one extra d20 and keep the highest result (2H20 if Unskilled; 3H20 if Skilled).
  * **Disadvantage (Dis):** Roll one fewer d20 (1d20 if Skilled; 2L20—roll two d20s and keep the lower result—if Unskilled).

#### Affliction 💀 (§3.4)

* **Affliction 💀:** An ongoing physiological, mental, or magical debilitating ailment or condition suffered by a target (character or monster):
  * **Defined Afflictions:** Poison, Disease, Death (via Death Checks), Burning, Weakness, Suffocation, Held, Immobilized, Stunned, Paralyzed, Frozen, Blinded, etc.
  * **The Zero-Duration Rule:** Afflictions rarely carry static multi-round duration strings; they persist until cured or saved against.
  * **Recovery Timing:** An afflicted character rolls immediately upon infliction, and on each of their Nishes 🚩 as an Act(F) until cured or dead. For monsters, the inflicting character rolls immediately upon infliction and on each of their Nishes.

#### Armor (AR 🧥) (§4.6)

* **AR 🧥 / Armor 🧥:** Protective equipment worn to absorb damage. When a character's Dodge 🏃 or Block 🛡️ check fails, they roll their exploding armor die (d4 to d12) and subtract the result from incoming Damage; the remaining damage becomes Wounds 🩸.

#### Artifact 🔮 (§6.3)

* **Artifact 🔮 (Legendary Exotic 🧿 Gear ⚙️):** Exceptionally rare, enchanted, or unique Exotic 🧿 treasures—such as ancient monoliths, divine masterworks, or arcane foci—discovered as quest or treasure loot. They exist outside the standard economy, cannot be bought in a store (Cost = "Artifact"), and always possess one or more Mods 🔌.

#### Attack (Atk ⚔️) (§4.4)

* **Atk ⚔️ (Attack):** Any offensive combat roll or strike made with a weapon, Power 🔥, or ability, rolled using Skilled: 2H20 + d(Atr) + Bonus or Unskilled: 1d20 + d(Atr) + Bonus vs. the target's static Defense. Attack d(Atr) dice explode.

#### Attribute (Atr ✅) (§2.1)

* **Atr ✅ (Attribute):** The 5 foundational attributes governing all physical and supernatural capabilities: Magic ✨, Might 💪, Mind 👁️, Motion 🏃, and Moxie 🫀 (often written ✨💪👁️🏃🫀 without labels). Attribute ratings equal polyhedral dice (4, 6, 8, 10, 12), and all abilities 🧠 and stats derive from them:
  * **Might 💪 (Mgt):** Brute force, melee weapons Atk and Dmg, armor size, Block Def, shields, and physical strength.
  * **Motion 🏃 (Mot):** Agility, Nish 🚩, movement, hurled weapons Atk and Dmg, Dodge, athletics, dexterity, balance, and sneaking.
  * **Mind 👁️ (Mnd):** Logic, knowledge, shot weapons Atk and Dmg, awareness, intelligence, persuasion, and deception.
  * **Magic ✨ (Mag):** Supernatural abilities, arcane channeling, powers, and mystical device operation.
  * **Moxie 🫀 (Mox):** Stamina, grit, Vitality ❤️, Death Checks, and physical endurance.

#### Bleeding (§4.7)

* At Vit 0 or below, a character suffers +1 Wound (-1 Vit) on each of their Nishes starting the round after entering Vit 0 or below (Bleeding does not apply upon first entering Vit 0 or below, and is not triggered by receiving more Wnds). Resolved first before Death Check and Unconscious Check until bandaged, stabilized with first aid, or healed magically.

#### Block (Blk 🛡️) (§4.5)

* **Blk 🛡️ (Block):** A physical melee weapon or shield defense roll using Might 💪. Melee Blocks work against any melee attack with Dmg at or below the weapon's Block Cap. A Shield Block works against any melee, hurled, or shot attack—or any other GM-approved "blockable" effect—with Dmg at or below the shield's Block Cap.
* **Block Cap 🛡️:** The maximum monster damage a melee weapon or shield can block.

#### Bolt ⚡ (§6.2)

* A concentrated energy reserve consisting of 5 accumulated Sparks (1⚡ = 5 Sparks). Expended to power high-impact 1-⚡ abilities or tactical pivots.

#### Breaking Melee (§4.3)

* Moving more than 1 square beyond an opponent's melee Atk range from an opponent who has engaged you in melee while that opponent has Nish in the round. Breaking Melee provokes an immediate Opportunity Attack.

#### Caret Notation (^) (§1.2.1)

* The foundational comparison syntax used because only players roll dice in SupaFlex: [PC Roll]^[Target Static Rating] or [Difficulty]. Because monsters never roll dice, all contests are framed as the player rolling against the opponent's static rating.

#### Critical 💀 (§1.5)

* A roll of natural 1 on a d20 during an Ability check. Triggers an automatic Critical fumble or consequences.

#### Damage (Dmg) (§4.6)

* **Dmg (Damage):** The raw amount of potential harm caused by an attack or ability before Armor (AR 🧥) reduction resolves it into Wounds 🩸.

#### Day (§3.1)

* **Day 📅:** A day in the character's in-game life (whereas a day in the player's real-world life is a Session).

#### Death Check 🫀 (§4.7)

* At Current Vitality 0 or below, characters resolve Negative Vit afflictions in order: 1. Bleeding (-1 Vit on Nish after first round), 2. Death Check (Resist Death 🫀 at 🫀^-Vit+5; e.g., at Vit 0 it is 🫀^5, at Vit -8 it is 🫀^13), and 3. Unconscious Check (Resist Unconsciousness 🫀 at 🫀^-Vit+10; e.g., at Vit 0 it is 🫀^10, at Vit -8 it is 🫀^18). Triggered upon reaching Vit 0 or below, anytime more Wnds are received, and on each of the character's Nishes starting next round.

#### Default Gear Possession (§5.4)

* **Default Possession Rule:** When a character learns or becomes skilled in a new Weapon ⚔️, Armor 🧥, or Shield 🛡️, the system assumes they possess the physical item as Gear ⚙️ (unless the GM determines otherwise based on campaign tone or situation).

#### Defense (Def) (§4.5)

* **Def (Defense):** The avoidance roll against an opponent's Atk rating to avoid damage. Defense is the master category offering the choice of either Dodge 🏃 (Dod) or Block 🛡️ (Blk).

#### Difficult Terrain (§4.3)

* Tactical battlefield squares (5x5 ft) that require 2 or more Movement Rate 👣 points to enter rather than the standard 1 point. The universal default cost of difficult terrain is 2 👣 per square unless designated otherwise.

#### Difficulty (Dif) (§1.2)

* **Dif (Difficulty):** A GM-assigned target number that the player's Ability Roll must tie or beat to succeed.

#### Disenchanting (§6.5)

* The process of breaking down magic items, exotics, or artifacts to harvest raw magical Essence, which is then used to forge or upgrade other equipment.

#### Dodge 🏃 (Def) (§4.5)

* An agile defense roll using Motion 🏃. Nearly always valid against melee, hurled, and shot attacks.

#### Duration (Dur ⏳) (§6.1)

* **Dur ⏳ (Duration):** The lifespan of an ability's active effect, stated in its Effect line if not obvious. Officially permitted durations are:
  * **Instant:** Resolves immediately with no lingering state.
  * **this Rnd:** Persists until the GM calls for new Nish rolls at the end of the round.
  * **1 Rnd (Universal Default):** Persists until the user's or monster's next round's Nish 🚩.
  * **Encounter:** Persists until the active encounter concludes.
  * **next …:** Persists until the specified upcoming roll or action occurs (e.g. next Def, next Atk, next Save).

#### Effect (§6.1)

* **Effect:** The concise mechanical rules string for using an ability or Exotic power.

#### Elements 🌟 (§2.3)

* **Element(s) 🌟:** The universal parent category encompassing all discrete, modular building blocks of SupaFlex (ALL Gear ⚙️ and ALL Abilities 🧠: Weapons, Armor, Shields, Supplies, Kits, Mods, Exotics, Artifacts, Skills, Traits, and Powers).

#### Encounter ⚔️ (§3.1)

* **Enc ⚔️ (Encounter):** Characters are ALWAYS in an Encounter, which comes in two types:
  * **Combat Encounter:** A self-contained tactical combat scene or high-stakes challenge measured in rounds, concluding when the GM declares threats neutralized.
  * **Roleplaying Encounter:** The distinct non-combat time span between Combat Encounters.

#### Essence (§6.5)

* The distilled magical currency harvested from disenchanted magical equipment, used for crafting, item upgrades, and enchanting.

#### Exotics 🧿 (§6.3)

* **Exotic(s) 🧿:** Any Gear ⚙️ with one or more Mods 🔌 (including all Artifacts 🔮 and modified weapons, armor, shields, or high-tech gear). Most Mods 🔌 grant one or more Exotic 🧿 Powers 🔥 that can be learned for 1 AP.

#### Exploding Die (§1.4)

* A die rolling its maximum face value (e.g. 8 on a d8) is rolled again, adding the new result to the running total. If the 2nd roll is also max face, the rule compounds and continues. All Attribute derived rolls (including the d(Atr) of Ability Rolls) explode indefinitely. Additionally: Focus, Damage, Armor, and all d👣 movement rolls (both Jumps and Running) are also all included as exploding rolls.

#### Fatigue Wounds 🩸 (§4.6)

* **Fatigue (Ftg) Wounds 🩸:** A minimal amount of baseline exhaustion and non-lethal bruising Wnds 🩸 a PC suffers if hit by a monster, regardless of the PC's AR 🧥 roll. Default monster Fatigue equals the monster Dmg ten's digit + 1 (e.g., Dmg 1–9 = 1, 10–19 = 2, 20–29 = 3). Fatigue wounds do not trigger wound-based afflictions (poison, disease) if armor absorbed all actual rolled damage.

#### Focus 🎯 (§2.2)

* **Focus 🎯:** A heroic resource die (d4 to d12) representing concentrated willpower and precision. Once per roll, after seeing the result, you may roll your Focus Die (exploding) and add it to the total. Focus steps down –1d (d12 ➔ d10 ➔ d8 ➔ d6 ➔ d4 ➔ Exhausted) only on a non-1, non-exploding roll, and steps up +1d on Flood triggers.

#### Free Ability (⭕) (§2.3)

* An innate baseline ability, weapon, or trait costing 0 AP.

#### Gear ⚙️ & Kits 📦 (§2.4)

* **Gear ⚙️:** The universal master category encompassing every physical item, tool, weapon, armor, shield, supply, or object an adventurer can carry, wear, or use—subdivided into Supplies 🎒, Weapons ⚔️, Armor 🧥, and Shields 🛡️ (priced in g/s, or found as loot for Artifacts 🔮). Exotics 🧿 are Gear with Mods 🔌.
* **Kits 📦:** Curated, pre-packaged gear bundles (e.g. Adventurer's Kit, Healer's Kit, Survival Kit, Trauma Kit) acquired for a single package price (g/s).

#### GM (Game Master)

* **GM 🔎 / 👑:** The person running the game, adjudicating rules, and guiding the story.

#### GM Intensity Die (§8.2)

* **Intensity Die (1d20):** A single d20 rolled by the GM for fast intensity checks, snap environmental adjudication, or quick summative NPC vs. NPC outcomes—one roll, then a full deterministic ruling.

#### Gold (g) & Silver (s) (§2.4)

* **g (Gold) —** Primary gold currency; 100 silver (s) = 1 gold (g).
* **s (Silver) —** Silver currency; 100s = 1g.

#### Hazard 💀 (§3.4)

* An environmental feature, trap, or terrain danger (pits, lava, fire walls, toxic clouds, icy ledges) that triggers an active roll or inflicts immediate damage and/or an Affliction.

#### Hit (§4.4)

* **Hit:** When an attack successfully lands (beats target Defense, or ties/beats on a PC attack).

#### Innate Path (§2.3)

* The foundational heritage path of all characters (includes Language Common for free Trait [0 AP], plus 1 AP learnable skills such as Nish 🚩, Resist Death 🫀, and Resist Unconsciousness 🫀).

#### Level ⭐ (§7.1)

* **Level ⭐ (Lvl):** A character's advancement milestone tier (gaining 2 AP 🧩 and a free Max Vit ❤️ roll per level).

#### Luck 🍀 (§2.2)

* **Luck 🍀: Heroic metacurrency (starting with 3, maximum pool of 5) awarded for creative, entertaining, or heroic play, spent to:**
  * **Reroll:** Reroll all dice involved in an in-game check, taking the new result (a roll may only be lucked once).
  * **Luck Twist:** Propose a creative twist to alter an ability (+1 Target, +1d, +1 Range Band, +1sq Push, etc.) with GM approval.
  * **Activate Any Power (1/Enc): Once per encounter, spend 1 Luck 🍀 to activate any known Power 🔥 (including Exotic 🧿 Powers 🔥), regardless of its Usage (1-🍀, 1-⚡, 1-Enc, 2-Enc, 3-Enc, or 1-Rnd).**

#### M/H/S (Melee, Hurled, Shot) (§5.1)

* **H, Hu (Hurled) —** Physically thrown weapons (daggers, axes, javelins, etc.).
* **M/H/S (Melee, Hurled, Shot) —** Shorthand for weapon types.
* **M, Me (Melee) —** Weapons swung or stabbed in close combat.
* **S, Sh (Shot) —** Ranged weapons that are shot, not thrown, (e.g. bows, crossbows, firearms).

#### Mods 🔌 (§6.3)

* **Mod(s) 🔌:** Modular modifications, hardware attachments, or enchantments (e.g. Joint Locks, Flood Lights, Undead Slayer Coating) belonging to Gear ⚙️, which makes that Gear an Exotic 🧿.

#### Movement Rate (MR 👣) (§4.3)

* **MR 👣 (Movement Rate):** A character's or monster's tactical movement allowance in 5-foot squares per Move (M) action, or jump distance as d👣.

#### Nish 🚩 (§4.2)

* **Nish 🚩 (Initiative):** A character's speed and readiness in combat, determining turn order each round.

#### Opportunity Attack ⚔️ (§4.4)

* **Opp Atk ⚔️ (Opportunity Attack):** An immediate melee-only reaction attack Act(F) executed when an engaged opponent moves more than 1 square out of melee range ("Breaking Melee") or takes a vulnerable non-melee action in melee reach:
  * **Melee Only:** Can only be made with a melee weapon in hand (or Brawl).
  * **Frequency:** May be made against each provoking opponent once per round.
  * **Vanilla Simplicity Rule:** The attacker cannot apply abilities, Focus 🎯, or Luck 🍀 to the attack or damage rolls (explosions still apply).

#### Out of Path (§2.3)

* Learning an element outside your character's active paths. Costs +2AP and requires GM approval.

#### Paths 🧭 (§2.3)

* **Path(s) 🧭:** The modular progression and identity suites that organize character capabilities—divided into Race Paths, Class Paths, Bonus/Specialization Paths, the Innate Path, and the open Universal Path (unlocked at character creation or via AP 🧩).

#### PC (Player Character)

* **PC:** A player-controlled character.

#### Player

* **Player 👤:** A person running a player character (not the GM).

#### Power 🔥 (§6.1)

* **Power 🔥:** An active ability (spell, psionic discipline, martial maneuver, or gear function) learned with AP 🧩 and defined by Action (AM, A, M, P, F), Usage (1-⚡, 1-🍀, 1-Enc, 2-Enc, 3-Enc, 1-Rnd), and Effect. Includes both Character 👤 Powers 🔥 and Exotic 🧿 Powers 🔥, which share identical syntax and rules.

#### Provoking (§4.3)

* Taking certain actions while engaged in melee (Breaking Melee, firing ranged weapons, casting spells at non-adjacent targets, using mundane items) that grants the adjacent opponent an Opportunity Attack.

#### Range Bands 🎯 (§4.8)

* **Rng 🎯 (Range Bands):** The 8 standardized tactical engagement distances: Self, Touch, 1sq, 2sq, 3sq, Short (Up to 6 sq), Medium (Up to 12 sq), and Long (Up to 24 sq). Attacking one band beyond listed range at Disadvantage is an optional rule (Extended Range; see Appendix A).

#### Resist (§4.5)

* A defensive saving throw using the most appropriate Attribute ✨💪👁️🏃🫀.

#### Round (Rnd) (§3.1)

* **Rnd (Round):** A single cycle in combat (~6 seconds) in which every participant takes their turn in Nish 🚩 order.

#### Rules

* **Rules:** The global SupaFlex game system rules, core mechanics, combat economy, and resolution engine.

#### Running / Sprinting (AM) (§4.3)

* Expending both Attack and Move allocations to sprint MR + d👣 squares on the ground or as a jump. All d👣 rolls explode.

#### Session (§3.1)

* **Session:** One real-world game session in the players' lives (whereas a day in the character's life is a Day 📅).

#### Sets 🗂️ (§3.2)

* **Sets 🗂️:** Pre-packaged collections of related abilities (Skill Sets, Weapon Skill Sets, Armor Skill Sets, Shield Skill Sets, Power Sets).
* **Skill Set 🎓:** A logical collection of related skills learned for 2 AP (In-Path) or 4 AP with GM approval (Out-of-Path).

#### Shields 🛡️ (§5.3)

* **Shields 🛡️:** Defensive Gear ⚙️ held in the off-hand to Block incoming attacks up to a Block Cap 🛡️. Shields can block all melee, hurled, and shot weapons, plus any other GM-approved "blockable" ability or hazard.

#### Skills 🎓 (§3.2)

* **Skill(s) 🎓:** Trained proficiencies that elevate an attribute-derived Ability Check from Unskilled (1d20 + d(Atr) + Bonus vs. Dif) to Skilled (2H20 + d(Atr) + Bonus vs. Dif).

#### Sparks (§6.2)

* Kinetic combat energy points accumulated during battle (via natural 20s, natural 1s, or exploding dice). Accumulating 5 sparks generates 1 Bolt ⚡. If an encounter ends with the character at full Bolt, then the Bolt and all Sparks are lost; otherwise, the number of current Sparks persist into the next encounter.

#### Square (sq) (§4.3)

* **sq (Square):** Tactical grid measurement where 1 square (or hex) equals 5 feet.

#### Stats

* **Stats:** All recorded mechanical values: Attributes (Atr), Vitality (Vit), Movement Rate (MR), Defense (Def), Attack (Atk), Block Cap, Actions, Usage, etc.

#### Stunned (§3.4)

* A debilitating affliction causing the target to lose their Attack (A) and Move (M) actions.

#### Supplies 🎒 (§2.4)

* **Supplies 🎒:** Standard adventuring equipment, tools, survival rations, containers, and field gear (priced in g/s)—literally ALL Gear ⚙️ that is not Weapons ⚔️, Armor 🧥, or Shields 🛡️.

#### Traits 🧬 (§2.3)

* **Trait(s) 🧬:** Inherent biological rules, physiological boons, and archetype perks. Traits are always active (passive) and use a Free (F) action.

#### Tremendous 🌟 (§1.5)

* **Tremendous (T) 🌟:** A natural 20 on any d20 during an Ability Roll, triggering a Tremendous narrative or tactical boon.

#### Universal Path 🧭 (§2.3)

* The baseline open capability path accessible to all characters. Elements on the Universal Path cost a flat 3 AP and require no GM approval.

#### Usage 🔄 (§6.2)

* **Usage 🔄:** The standardized 6-option activation frequency limit of an ability: 1-⚡ (1 Bolt), 1-🍀 (1 Luck), 1-Enc, 2-Enc, 3-Enc, or 1-Rnd.

#### Vitality (Vit / ❤️) (§4.7)

* **Vit ❤️ (Vitality): A character's or monster's total health pool reduced by Wounds 🩸. Reaching 0 Vit or below triggers Bleeding (on Nish starting next round), Death Checks (Resist Death 🫀^-Vit+5), and Unconscious Checks (Resist Unconsciousness 🫀^-Vit+10).**

#### Weakness (§3.4)

* An ongoing affliction causing all of the target's active rolls (Attacks, Defenses, Saves) to have Disadvantage, and halving their Movement Rate (👣, rounded up). Weakened has no effect on rolled Damage (Dmg ) or Armor (AR 🧥).

#### Weapon (Wpn) ⚔️ (§5.1)

* **Wpn ⚔️ (Weapon):** Offensive Gear ⚙️ (Melee 💪, Hurled 🏃, or Shot 👁️).

#### Wounds (Wnd / 🩸) (§4.6)

* **Wnd 🩸 (Wounds):** Cumulative injury points suffered by a character or monster and tracked directly against Vitality ❤️. Wounds equal the Damage not stopped by Armor (AR 🧥), or Damage that bypasses AR 🧥.

## Appendix D · Quick Reference Sheet

**Quick Reference:** Combat Turn & Core Mechanics

* **Round Action Pool —** Every combat round, you have 1 Attack Act(A), 1 Move Act(M), 1 Partial Act(P), and unlimited Free Act(F) actions.
* **Running Strike / Sprint —** Combine your Attack and Move Act(AM) for large powerful actions.
* **Core Rolls —** Unskilled = 1d20 + d(Atr) | Skilled = 2H20 + d(Atr) (roll two d20s, keep highest).
* **Tremendous & Critical —** Tremendous (T) 🌟- Natural 20 grants narrative triumph; Critical (C) - 💀 Natural 1 causes tactical setback.
* **Defenses —** Dodge 🏃 (vs any attack); Block 🛡️ (within Block Cap); Resist (vs magic/mental and more). Armor (AR) 🧥 rolls if Dodge or Block fails!
* **Difficulty Ladder —** Easy 10 · Medium 15 · Hard 20 · Very Hard 25 · Extreme 28+ · ties go to PCs (§1.2).
* **Advantage / Disadvantage —** +1 d20 keep highest / –1 d20 (2L20 if Unskilled); never stacks (§1.3).
* **Focus 🎯 —** add after seeing the roll, once per roll; refills on Flood triggers (§2.2).
* **Luck 🍀 — reroll an entire roll once, spend on a Luck Twist, or once per encounter activate any known Power 🔥 regardless of Usage (§2.2).**
* **Damage — Dmg – AR 🧥 = Wounds 🩸; at 0 Vit or below resolve in order: Bleeding, Death Check (🫀^-Vit+5), Unconscious Check (🫀^-Vit+10) (§4.6–4.7).**
* **Usage 🔄 —** 1-⚡, 1-🍀, 1-Enc, 2-Enc, 3-Enc, 1-Rnd (§6.2).
* **Range 🎯 —** Self, Touch, 1sq, 2sq, 3sq, Short (up to 6 sq), Medium (up to 12 sq), Long (up to 24 sq) (§4.8).
* **Spending AP 🧩 —** In-Path 1 AP · Skill Set 2 AP · Universal 3 AP · Out-of-Path 3 AP + GM · New Path 4 AP + GM (§7.3).
